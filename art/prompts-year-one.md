# Year One: Higgsfield prompts

One year after the Sleep. Stylized animated look: cartoon-like but adult, anime-adjacent, not anime.
Descriptions follow the updated ElevenLabs character prompts (flow swXwYpOym9CTRn4zYWDQ).
Names: chapter names first, bible names in brackets.

## Notes from the first Higgsfield pass (2026-11-02)
- Everyone looked too young: STYLE now casts them like twenty-something actors playing teens.
- Juno: toned, prettier, brighter eyes. Tock: taller, lighter skin. Fen: wild self-cut shag, no fade. Kit: prettier, toned, longer shaggy wolf cut.

- Builds (2026-11-02): the girls are athletic. Juno like a volleyball player, Lyra like a track runner or dancer, Kit lean and wiry. Fit but believable for teens: no six-packs or carved abs.

## How to use
1. Paste STYLE before every prompt. Paste NEGATIVE into the negative field.
2. Generate the character sheets first. Use each picked sheet as the reference image for that character's scenes.
3. Sheets 16:9. Daily-life and fight scenes 16:9 or 3:2. Group shots 21:9.

## STYLE (prefix, never changes)
Stylized animated-film key art for a mature YA graphic novel. Bold confident ink linework, clean graphic shapes, flat colour with soft cel shading and a light painterly texture. Grounded adult proportions, expressive but realistic faces, Western animated-feature feel. Cast older, like a live-action teen drama where the actors are in their early twenties: mature faces, defined jawlines and cheekbones, adult builds, nothing childlike. Palette: humid tropical green, dust-orange, sea blue and sun-bleached neutrals, hazy Sahara-dust light. World: a small Caribbean island one year after every adult fell asleep; the teenagers run everything now. No text, no logos, no watermarks.

## OUTFIT RULE (add to every character prompt)
Clothes made by the island's teenage tailor: practical tropical streetwear stitched from salvage. Sun-faded sailcloth and canvas, carnival-costume fabric used as trims and linings, car seatbelt webbing for straps and belts, inner-tube rubber patches on knees and elbows, fishing-net mesh vent panels, hand-stitched contrast seams, loose and breathable for heat, durable, deliberately cool, not ragged. Each person has one hand-painted colour stripe on their clothes.

## NEGATIVE
anime, manga, chibi, oversized eyes, glossy 3D render, photorealistic, plastic skin, ragged post-apocalyptic rags, gas masks, leather fetish gear, text, logos, watermark, extra fingers, deformed hands

## 1. Character sheets (16:9)
Suffix for every sheet: Character turnaround sheet: full body front, three-quarter and back views plus one head close-up, plain warm-grey background, even light.

**Juno [Imani], 18, the medic.** Spanish-Caribbean girl, short-ish, toned and athletic with defined arms, light-brown skin, very pretty, hair up in a puff, round wire glasses, bright alert eyes with a warm lift to them, focused and capable, a little tired but very much alive. Wears a sleeveless sand-coloured sailcloth work vest over a long-sleeve sun shirt, a wide apron-belt of seatbelt webbing with pouches for gauze, scissors and a thermometer, cropped cargo trousers, tyre-tread sandals. A school exercise book on a cord at her hip, worn soft. Personal stripe: red, painted across one shoulder like a medic's band.

**Tock [Danny], 17, fire.** Spanish-Caribbean boy, Juno's brother, very tall and lanky, long limbs, light-brown skin (lighter than you'd guess), buzz cut, a small white nick through his left eyebrow, jaw set. Wears a sleeveless heavy canvas shirt cut like a welder's apron, scorch marks around the hem, forearm guards made from leather car-seat upholstery, loose work shorts with reinforced knees, steel-toe boots. Faint heat-shimmer around his hands. Personal stripe: orange, half burned off.

**Fen [Kai], 17, air (stillness).** Indo-Caribbean boy, thin, wild shaggy mullet he cuts himself, uneven and overgrown, no fade, falling over his eyes, one earphone in and the other hanging loose, eyes down and to the side. Wears an oversized faded band t-shirt under a lightweight hooded sailcloth over-shirt with many small pockets, a chest harness of seatbelt webbing holding a handheld boat radio and coiled antenna wire, slim cargo trousers, worn sneakers. Personal stripe: sea blue, down one sleeve.

**Lyra [Asha], 17, air (rush).** Indo-Caribbean girl, Fen's twin with the same face but all motion, long hair tied up and swinging. Wears a fitted cropped top with a carnival-sequin trim, high-waisted stretch cargo trousers built for running and jumping, fishing-net mesh panels at the sides, both wrists wrapped in tape, one wrist with an old healed-break brace, light shoes. Mid-step, about to leap. Personal stripe: magenta, zig-zag.

**Sol [Tori], 17, ice.** Chinese-Caribbean girl, round and solid, short practical hair. Wears an old football club shirt (no crest or text) under a sleeveless insulated vest stitched from a cooler-bag lining, quilted, loose shorts, ankle socks and sliders. A faint frost on her fingertips, a cold breath in warm air, soft open face not quite meeting the viewer's eye. Personal stripe: pale ice blue.

**Lonny [Lonny], 18, strength.** Afro-Caribbean boy, very tall and broad, easy handsome face, grinning. Wears a sleeveless top with the arm holes torn wider, a carrying harness of seatbelt webbing and cargo straps across his back for water drums, heavy patched canvas shorts with inner-tube rubber knee pads, work boots. Dust on his arms, a small nosebleed he hasn't noticed. Personal stripe: forest green.

**Kit [Nia], 16, life and decay.** Caucasian-Caribbean girl, small and sharp, toned and defined, strikingly pretty, shoulder-length shaggy wolf-cut hair, nose piercing, guarded eyes. Wears her father's old pressed work shirt, re-cut by the tailor, sleeves rolled, over a cropped tank, a gardener's apron of canvas with seed pouches, loose trousers rolled at the ankle, bare dirty palms. A single key on a cord around her neck. Head tilted as if listening to something under the ground. Personal stripe: moss green, faded.

**Judge [Cole Mayers], 18, the sea.** Afro-Caribbean boy, a fisherman's son, lean and weathered, close-cropped hair, steady unreadable face looking straight at the viewer. Wears a salt-stained short-sleeve shirt open at the collar under a cut-down yellow oilskin fisherman's jacket, sleeves removed, rubber boots, a webbing belt with a boat radio clipped on and a holstered pistol he never touches. Personal stripe: none; he wears the Harbour's black armband.

**The Gardener, 16.** Afro-Caribbean boy from the hills, barefoot with earth on his feet, a plain white shirt and plain trousers, untailored, the only person not in the tailor's clothes. Fine pale thread-like marks at his hairline and wrists, calm gentle smile, head slightly bowed, hands open. Green shoots growing from the ground around his feet.

**Dela, 16, the tailor (new character).** Afro-Caribbean girl from the road below the bend, apprenticed at her aunt's carnival mas camp before the Sleep. Bold, stylish, measuring tape around her neck, pins in her sleeve, thimble on a cord. Wears the best version of everyone else's look: a sailcloth jacket with carnival-sequin lining showing at the cuffs, utility belt of scissors and thread spools, wide cargo trousers, tyre-tread sandals she made herself. Personal stripe: every colour, a little of each.

## 2. Powers, day to day (16:9)

**Ice for the fish hold.** Sol kneels in the open hold of a small fishing boat at the Harbour dock, both palms flat on a pile of fish, frost spreading in a ring across the scales, steam of cold air rising, dock kids in Harbour armbands watching from above, one of them handing her a water bottle. She looks exhausted and proud.

**The kettle.** Tock in a dim concrete kitchen at night holding a dented steel pot in his bare hands, the water inside at a rolling boil, a baby asleep in a sling against his chest, oven gloves with chilli prints hanging unused on the stove behind him. Warm orange glow from his palms, careful face.

**The weld.** Tock crouched at a barricade of cars and corrugated galvanize across a hillside road bend, pressing a glowing hand along the seam of a steel quarry gate, sparks and molten drips, Lonny holding the gate upright one-handed, Fen with his palms out keeping it perfectly still. Late afternoon dust light.

**The water run.** Lonny walking up a steep dusty hill road at dawn with two blue 200-litre water drums on his shoulders, a line of small kids behind him carrying jugs, the barricade at the top of the road, his harness straps creaking, grinning at something a kid said.

**The garden on the quarry floor.** Kit kneeling in a pale, blasted-rock quarry turned into a vegetable garden, her palm flat on the soil, seedlings visibly leaning toward her hand, a pile of scraps beside her rotted to rich black compost, a rainwater pond behind her gone green. Fen sitting on a rock nearby with the radio, watching her, not the garden.

**The ward.** Juno on her knees in a front room full of sleeping adults on mattresses in rows, holding a teaspoon to an old woman's lips, an IV bag hanging from a wire coat hanger on a curtain rail, her notebook open on the floor full of numbers, Sol behind her laying a cold cloth on someone's forehead, morning light through louvre windows.

**The radio roof.** Fen on a flat concrete rooftop at night, cross-legged among antenna wire and car batteries, handheld radio to his mouth, the black island below with one lit house, a navy ship's lights on the horizon, a small drone with a red light hovering above him that he hasn't seen yet.

## 3. Fights (16:9 or 21:9)

**Night raid at the bend.** Night, a barricade of cars at a bend in a hill road. Raiders in carnival costume pieces (feathered shoulder plates, wire, sequins) climbing over the cars with cutlasses. Tock grabbing one raider's cutlass blade with a glowing hand so the metal goes red; Lonny throwing a car door like a shield; Kit on a gallery behind them with a shotgun, scared and steady. Torchlight, smoke, dust.

**The twins.** A dusty road between small concrete houses. Lyra mid-air, spinning, her kick blurred with a burst of wind that throws two masked raiders off their feet; Fen standing completely still in front of her taking a full punch to the chest without moving, the air around him rippling. The two of them moving like a dance. Kids watching from doorways.

**Frozen road.** The siege, night, rain. Dock boys with guns and Harbour armbands charging up a wet hill road toward a welded gate; Sol on her knees behind the gate with both hands on the wet ground, ice racing outward across the road in branching white veins, the front attackers slipping and falling. Lightning crackling along the gate's wire from a boy with glowing hands in the background.

**Judge on the water.** A small fishing boat in a choppy bay at dusk. Judge standing at the bow with one hand open, the sea around his boat suddenly glass-flat while a raiding pirogue nearby is being swamped by a standing wave, his crew gripping the gunwales, his face calm and unreadable.

## 4. Group shots (21:9)

**The Trace, one year on.** Wide shot at golden hour of a dead-end hill road of ordinary concrete houses with black water tanks on stands, a barricade wall of cars and galvanize at the bend, a garden on the pale quarry floor above. The group on the barricade in their tailored clothes: Juno with her notebook, Tock sitting on a car roof, Lonny leaning on a water drum, Sol cross-legged, Fen with the radio, Lyra balanced on the wall's edge, Kit a little apart, Dela fixing Lonny's strap. The sea and Morne Bay town below in dust haze.

**The fitting.** Inside a cluttered carnival mas camp workshop, bolts of sequinned fabric, mannequins, sewing machines run off a car battery. Dela pinning a new jacket onto Tock while he stands awkwardly with his hands held away from the cloth, Sol and Lyra trying on pieces and laughing, Juno checking the pockets fit her scissors, coloured stripes being painted onto sleeves.
