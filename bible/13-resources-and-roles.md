# 13. Resources and roles

Everybody has a role. That is the theme as a survival fact: a community that thrives is one where every niche is held. This file is the ledger of what the island has, what runs out when, and who does what. Chapters must honour these numbers.

## Fuel

The thing [Fen](cast/fen.md) needed on night one, and the thing that decides year one.

| Source | How much | Who holds it | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gas station, Morne Bay | Underground tanks, tens of thousands of litres | The Harbour | Pumps need power. Hand pump or bucket-and-hose from the fill ports. Judge posts guards on it by week one. |
| Fishing fleet diesel | The dock tanks, plus what's in each boat | The Harbour | Diesel keeps better than petrol. Boats are the island's most valuable machines. |
| Cars | 40 to 60 litres each, hundreds of cars | Whoever gets there with a hose | Siphoning is the first skill every kid learns. Anti-siphon valves on newer cars; older cars are gold. |
| Trace | 2 jerry cans at the Quills', a few more scattered, lawnmower petrol | The Trace | The Quill generator burns about 1.5 litres an hour. Two cans is a night. |
| Propane | Cooking gas cylinders in every kitchen; the gas truck's load | Everyone, then the Harbour | Cooking, and later, with a mechanic, engines. |

Petrol degrades in months. By year two most petrol engines are dead and diesel is the only thing that runs. Solar panels on the hotels and the clinic become the Trace's long game.

## Cars and machines

Cars are a commodity, not transport. Fuel, batteries, parts, and a place to sleep that locks.

- **Mechanics are nobility.** Any kid who can keep a generator or an outboard running eats first in every faction. The Trace has none at the start. [Fen](cast/fen.md) learns, badly, from his father's manuals. The Harbour has two, apprentices from the garage on the coast road.
- **Batteries** are the second currency. Car batteries run radios, lights, and the oxygen concentrator. Charging them needs the generator, solar, or a Lightning kid holding the terminals. The Harbour has four Lightning kids. The Trace has none. That is the single biggest resource gap on the island, and why Fen's long game is solar panels off the hotel roofs.
- **Trucks move water.** The Hilux that took the six to the beach on day zero is the Trace's most important vehicle.
- **Boats** are the Harbour's whole power. A boat can fish, trade, and (people believe) reach the ships.

## Food

| Source | Who | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pantries | Everyone, for two to three weeks | The Mosses have a month. The Pierres' flat had four days. |
| Fish | The Harbour | The island's protein. Judge's whole leverage. Spoils in a day without ice. |
| Ice | [Sol](cast/sol.md) | Ice turns a day's catch into a week's food. The most valuable gift on the island. |
| Crops | The Hills | Cassava, plantain, dasheen, breadfruit, mangoes in season. Months to grow. The Trace's quarry-floor garden is year one's gamble. |

**Holes in the Trace**: nobody can cook for thirty, nobody can fish, nobody can grow anything, nobody can slaughter a goat, nobody can fix a car. Every hole is a plot: either someone learns, someone is brought in from outside, or the Trace trades for it and pays in something that matters.

## The rule for chapters

Before anything with an engine runs, somebody found fuel. Before anybody eats, somebody caught, grew, traded or stole it. Before a sleeper lives another week, somebody did the work. If the chapter skips the cost, add it back.
