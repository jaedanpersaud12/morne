# 09. Relationships

How the people pull on each other, and how the world pulls on that. The world changes relationships (a gate closes, a sleeper is rooted, food runs short) and relationships change the world (a crush opens a channel to the Harbour, a secret brings Judge to the hill). Every shift below is caused by something in [13](13-resources-and-roles.md), [08](08-factions-and-politics.md) or [05](05-network-and-gardener.md), never by mood.

## Rules

- **Nobody is "the love interest."** Everyone who is loved is also a person with a job, a fault line and a family in the ground.
- **Romance costs something.** Every relationship that crosses a wall (Trace and town, Trace and Harbour, living and rooted) makes somebody's life harder. If it doesn't, cut it.
- **No triangle for its own sake.** Two people who want the same person is only on the page if the world made it matter.
- **The world gets in the way, always.** A kiss in year one is interrupted by a water run. That is the genre.
- **Friendships break for reasons, not for drama.** Every break has a cause in the ledger: a death, a secret, a resource, a conversion.
- **Nothing is cheap to repair.** A friendship that breaks over a rooted sleeper does not come back with an apology. It comes back with an act.
- **YA.** Teenagers. Attraction, kissing, jealousy, heartbreak. Nothing explicit, ever.

## The core relationships and where they go

### Fen and Kit: the taxi

**Day zero:** already real, and secret. Late nights in Ambrose's parked taxi at the bend, radio low, where nobody on the Trace was watching. Eight months, which is long enough. He is the reason she is half in the group. She is part of why he is still alive, and he has never said so to anyone, including her.

| Phase | What the world does | What it does to them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night one | Her father falls; his mother is at peace | He goes to the roof instead of to her. First failure. |
| Month one | She roots Ambrose. The group splits. | He is between Juno and Kit and chooses neither out loud. She notices. |
| Months 3 to 6 | The twins awaken; the dance | The first time she sees him move, the secret stops being keepable. The group finds out by watching his face. |
| Year one end | The Gardener asks for Kit by name | Fen is the one who wants to shut the gate. First time he and Kit want opposite things. |
| Year two | She hears the dead; the pull | She drifts toward the Hills. He can't follow where she's going. The relationship becomes the question of whether you can love someone the island is taking. |
| Year three | Open | Either he lets her go the way he let his mother go, or he goes with her. The book's quiet climax. |

**Rule:** they never have a scene where they explain their feelings. They have scenes where they do things for each other and the reader works it out.

### Juno and Fen: the edge

Not romance. The deepest friendship in the book, and the one readers will want to be romance. Don't give it to them. Both have been to the edge; they recognise it without saying it. He is the one person she doesn't perform for. She is the one person he explains himself to.

**Breaks:** when she finds out he gave her name to Judge on night one, and that he waited to tell her. That is the trust break of year one. It mends when he tells her the thing from the radio (the quarantine is right) before anyone else.

### Juno and Judge: enemies to something

The enemies-to-lovers slot, and it has to be earned over two books or it's a cliché.

| Phase | Where it is |
| --- | --- |
| Night one | A name on a radio. "The Vale girl. From the paper." |
| Month one | She goes down the hill to treat his kids because somebody has to. He watches her work. He is the first person in her life who needs what she knows and says so. |
| Months 3 to 12 | Trade. He rations, taxes, bargains for Sol. She hates what he does and understands why. He is the only person on the island who runs numbers the way she does. |
| Year two | The deal with the Gardener. She can't forgive the rows of rooted sleepers at the fish market. He can't forgive that the Trace's thirty eat while his two hundred ration. The attraction is real and neither will act on it, because acting on it would be choosing a side. |
| Year three | Open. The honest ending is that it never becomes what readers want, and both of them know what it could have been. |

**Rule:** he never lies to her. That is the whole basis of it. Everyone else manages her; Judge tells her the number.

### Sol and Lux: across the wall

Sol is gay and out to no one ([tori.md](cast/sol.md)). Lux is a Harbour girl whose family worked the ice plant that Sol's family owned. Sol's power now does the job Lux's family used to do.

- **Year one:** Sol has reasons to go to the Harbour (ice for the fish hold, trade), and one real reason. The group thinks she is being loyal, or being used. Tock thinks she's passing information. She is not.
- **The doubt beat:** when the Harbour knows something it shouldn't, the Trace looks at Sol. She can't explain why she was there without telling them who she is. She chooses the accusation over the truth. That is the chapter.
- **Lux's answer:** open, and it should not be a clean yes. Lux has a family in a strongman's town and a faith of her own. The world gets in the way.
- **The group's answer:** the thing that costs. Who says the wrong thing (Tock, probably, and he means it as a joke). Who says the right thing first (Olli, who doesn't know it's a thing). Who takes longest (open).

### Lonny and the girl on the road

Her name is open ([16](16-open-questions.md)). She lives on the road below the bend, which means she is outside whatever the Trace decides "inside" means.

- **Day one:** he is the one who asks for the road to be checked. She is alive, with a sleeping mother and a small brother.
- **Month one:** the gate question. The Trace does not take her in. Lonny takes food out. The ledger notices.
- **Months 3 to 6:** he leaves. Goes to live on the road, or in town. The friend who walks out. Tock does his runs alone and it nearly kills him.
- **Months 6 to 12:** he comes back, or she comes in, or neither. The strongest kid on the island choosing a person over a wall is the Trace's lesson about what walls are for.

### Lyra and the convert

Lyra dates someone in the Trace. Cass, the older boy who resents Juno. It starts as Lyra being Lyra, with everyone, loudly. It becomes serious at the wrong time. When Cass loses his mother to a bed death, he blames Juno, converts to the Gardener's way, and carries sleepers out through the gate at night. Lyra finds out because she feels it through Fen, who saw. She has to choose between her boyfriend and her twin, and the choice is not close, and it still breaks her.

### Tock and the baby

Not a romance. The Pell baby he walked past and went back for. He raises her. Nobody decides this; it happens. It is the relationship that keeps him from becoming what his anger could make him. When the baby gets sick in the dry season, the whole Trace sees what Tock is capable of, and some of them are afraid.

### Tock and Ida

Friction, not romance, at least in book one. Ida refuses to root her father on religious grounds and is the loudest voice against Kit. Tock's grandmother called his anger sinful; Ida talks the way Granny talked. He hates her and listens to her, and she is the only person in the Trace who tells him his power is not a sin.

### Juno and Kit: the argument

The central friendship-break of book one. Juno was the one who said Kit's name first, every day of the eight months Kit had lived at the bend. Then Kit rooted her father without asking, and it worked, and it made Juno's medicine look like cruelty.

- Neither is wrong. That is what makes it a break and not a fight.
- They stop speaking. The group routes around it. Fen carries messages and hates it.
- It mends with an act: Juno carries a sleeper to the garden herself, at night, alone, and Kit finds her there. Which sleeper is open. Granny would be the biggest choice.

### Juno and Tock: the siblings

Never breaks, and they fight constantly. He is the only person she hits. She is the only person who can talk him down. When she passes the age line, or doesn't, he is the one in the room.

### Fen and Lyra: the unbreakable one

The bond that holds. They agree on their mother. They feel each other when linked. They fight, and the weather changes, and they still have each other. In a book full of fractures the reader needs one thing that doesn't break. This is it. **Do not break it for drama.** The only thing that could is the network, in year three.

## Doubt: who the group stops trusting, and why

| Who | Why the group doubts them | The truth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fen | He talks to Judge on the radio every night | He is managing the most dangerous person on the island and telling no one, because telling them means explaining night one |
| Sol | She keeps going to the Harbour | Lux |
| Kit | She hears things. She rooted her father. She talks to Moth. | She is becoming what the island needs, and she is still Kit, and both are true |
| Lonny | He takes food down past the barricade | He is in love with someone the Trace won't let in |
| Cass | He is angry at Juno | He is right about some of it, and the Gardener got to him first |
| Juno | She decides who gets water | She is right, and being right is expensive |

## How the world moves relationships (quick reference)

| World event | Relationship it changes |
| --- | --- |
| The generator runs all night | Judge finds the hill; Fen meets Judge |
| Kit roots Ambrose | Juno/Kit break; Fen in the middle |
| Six bags of fluid | Juno becomes the one who decides; Cass turns |
| Dry season, water carried from the Hills | Lonny indispensable, then gone |
| Sol's ice becomes currency | Sol at the Harbour; the doubt; Lux |
| The first bed death | Cass converts; Lyra chooses |
| The twins awaken | Fen/Kit secret ends; the group sees Fen dance |
| Judge's deal with the Gardener | Juno/Judge can never happen |
| The Gardener asks for Kit | Fen wants the gate shut; first time he and Kit want opposite things |
| The pull | Sol and Kit drift; the Trace empties from inside |

## How relationships move the world

- Fen's love for Kit is why she is half in the group, which is why the Trace has a bridge to the Hills.
- Fen saying Juno's name on night one is why Judge comes to the hill.
- Sol's crush opens the only honest channel between the Trace and the Harbour.
- Lonny's girlfriend is why the gate question is never settled.
- Juno and Judge understanding each other is why the trade works for a year, and why the siege, when it comes, is personal.
- Lyra and Cass is how the Gardener gets inside the walls.

## The group's language as a barometer

When the six are joking, the Trace is fine. When the goat stops being funny, something has broken. When a joke dies with someone, nobody makes it again until the day someone does, and that is the day the group starts to heal. See [14](14-writing-rules.md#the-groups-private-language).
