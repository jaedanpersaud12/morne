# 23. The outside world

What the world beyond the quarantine line knows, does and says, and what the kids can see of it. **Canon vs new:** anything linked to a chapter or bible page is on the page or locked. Items tagged `(new)` are proposals for Jaedan to accept, change or strike. The rule from [05](05-network-and-gardener.md#what-it-wants) holds: the quarantine is right and the navy is not cruel. It is careful, and from a roof careful looks the same as cruel.

## What the outside world knows and believes

Kept to [02](02-the-sleep.md#the-boundary-a-quarantine-not-a-dome): detected within a day; ships, drones, drops that stop; adults who land sleep.

**How it was noticed (new).** The Monday ferry doesn't sail, the island's coast guard and air traffic channels go silent, and diaspora calls ring out. On commercial night-time satellite imagery the island goes black at 12:14 except for one point on the south hill, the Quill house ([ch 3](../chapters/ch03-signal/LATEST.md) L189). The neighbouring islands, under the same dust, find their adults awake, so the cause is on the island and not in the air.

**Who runs it (new).** The island is a small independent state ("this island and its territorial waters", [ch 6](../chapters/ch06-sixteen/LATEST.md) L89; Juno is "top of the country", [Juno](cast/juno.md)). Its government is asleep. The senior official still awake is its UN ambassador, in New York that Sunday. Within 48 hours WHO declares a public health emergency of international concern, and a multinational task force follows under a UN mandate, using the navies that already patrol the Caribbean. The first ship on station is European, which is why Fen can't place the accent. Each ship enforces twelve miles around *itself* (L89), so the line is a ring of overlapping circles, not a wall.

**What the scientists learn (new).** Week 1: adults off the island are fine, and drone footage shows children moving. Movement 2: the two men landed on the north beach are asleep by sunset ([17](17-book-one-outline.md#parallel-stories-the-world-off-page)), and nobody lands again. The drones carry air samplers, which is why the ch 6 drone hung so low and so still. Movement 3: in the lab, cultured spores put adult animals to sleep and grow harmlessly into young animals' nerve tissue. That is where the word "carriers" comes from. They get the mechanism and never learn that the network wants anything.

**The official story (new).** "An acute mass-somnolence event of unknown origin"; the population is "stable". For weeks the briefings avoid the word *children*. "Assistance is being coordinated" (L89) is policy language that promises nothing.

**Media (new).** No press can get in. Journalists who hire boats on the neighbouring island are stopped at the line. What gets out is leaked drone footage, and one frame goes round the world: a bedsheet on a white quarry floor, *24 KIDS. SEND HELP* ([ch 6](../chapters/ch06-sixteen/LATEST.md) L151). The Trace becomes the island's face abroad and never knows it.

**The diaspora (new).** More islanders live abroad than at home, in Toronto, New York and London, and the adults who were off-island on day zero can't go back. Families register their children's names and streets with consulates, and the lists become the world's census of the awake. "Bring them home" runs up against a louder "not here". Governments whose tourists' children are in the Shore hotels push hardest for exceptions: the root of "if the outside world offers to take one kid off the island, who goes?" ([10](10-conflicts.md#moral-questions-with-no-clean-answer)).

**Juno's father.** Awake in Toronto. Juno gets his voicemail and hangs up before the beep ([ch 2](../chapters/ch02-count/LATEST.md) L27). He calls back after 3 a.m., into a dead network ([ch 4](../chapters/ch04-ice/LATEST.md) L37) (new). "The call he doesn't understand" ([17](17-book-one-outline.md#parallel-stories-the-world-off-page)) is a consular officer on Monday asking for his children's names and street (new). He gives them, which is how "the outside world knows exactly where his children are" ([supporting](cast/supporting.md#offshore)) and how the scientist has his name in ch 22.

## The rules of the line

The broadcast says three things: stay in port, stay twelve miles off, or "will be stopped" ([ch 6](../chapters/ch06-sixteen/LATEST.md) L89, L97). Judge reads it right: "The ship is to keep us here" (L165).

| If | Then |
| --- | --- |
| A boat heads out | Hailed on 16, then loudhailer, flares, a drone or helicopter overhead (new). Most turn back here; Zu always does ([supporting](cast/supporting.md#the-shore)) |
| It keeps coming | Warning shots, then disabling fire into the engine (new). Nobody boards, ever. A dead boat is left to the current |
| It still won't stop | It sinks, and the rescue faction dies with it ([11](11-timeline.md#months-6-to-12), [08](08-factions-and-politics.md)) |
| A drone is shot at | No return fire; the navy never fires at land (new). The drone is replaced the next day and the shooter's position is logged |
| Someone signals | Logged, photographed, never answered. As policy there is no two-way contact, because any answer is a promise (new). The sheet got a drone and will never get a reply |
| An adult lands | Asleep within hours, left where they fall ([02](02-the-sleep.md)) |

## Contact so far (by ch 8)

- None both ways. The navy has spoken once, on a loop, to "all stations". In Fen's words, "They're not talking to us" ([ch 6](../chapters/ch06-sixteen/LATEST.md) L125).
- Fen knows it is a quarantine. Judge knows and has told 230 kids (L163). Juno told the road "Somebody's coming" (L141), and Tock believes her ([ch 7](../chapters/ch07-heat/LATEST.md) L103).
- The drone has seen the lit house and probably the sheet. Fen has told no one ([Fen](cast/fen.md)).
- No drops yet. Judge's five weeks of rice assumes no help ([ch 8](../chapters/ch08-market/LATEST.md) L161).

## Questions the book never answers

- **Could the world have saved them?** The quarantine is right ([05](05-network-and-gardener.md)), and it is still a choice made by people who went home at night. The book leaves it open, the way it leaves open whether a group can survive without a predator ([01](01-premise-and-theme.md#the-question-we-never-answer)).
- **What the network is.** Offshore gets the mechanism, never the meaning ([05](05-network-and-gardener.md#what-stays-unexplained)). No expert explains the dream.
- **Does anyone out there see them as people?** The voice stays somewhere between officer and specimen-collector ([supporting](cast/supporting.md#offshore)).
- **What her father would have said.** The voicemail is never left.

## Conflicts found

1. **Phones.** [02](02-the-sleep.md#infrastructure-in-order-of-failure) says the cell network fails on day 2 to 3, but in [ch 4](../chapters/ch04-ice/LATEST.md) L37 Sol has had *No Service* since about 3 a.m. on night one. The chapter wins; the 02 table needs changing.
2. **Juno and the ship.** [14](14-writing-rules.md#decisions-cost) writes as if the bill has already arrived ("Juno hears about the ship from Judge"), yet in ch 8 Judge spends the whole afternoon with her and never mentions it. That fits the outline ("ch 10 at the latest") but not 14's wording. Possible reason: in ch 6 (L175) Judge said she'd come down once she knew, so when she comes he assumes she does.
3. **Broken link** in 02: `imani.md` should be `cast/juno.md`.
4. **Sovereignty.** The island's name is open ([06](06-island.md)), but "territorial waters" and "top of the country" make it a state, and this page assumes it is independent. If it is a territory, the governing power runs the quarantine instead of a UN mandate.
