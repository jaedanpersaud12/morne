# 24. Glossary

The first two parts are canon: everything in them is on the page in chapters 1 to 8 or stated in the bible, with a source. Numbers after a chapter link are line numbers in that chapter's `LATEST.md` (2 November 2026). Meanings are given only where the chapters or bible give them. The last part, [Words the book could use](#words-the-book-could-use), is new: suggestions, every one tagged `(new)`, for Jaedan to take or leave. Spelling and dialect rules for all of these words are in [21](21-style-sheet.md#dialect-spelling).

## The group's private language

The rules are in [14](14-writing-rules.md#the-groups-private-language): Kit is the lens; never explain a joke on the spot; the language is a survival tool on an open radio; when the jokes stop, things are bad, and when a joke dies with someone, the first person to make it again starts the healing ([09](09-relationships.md#the-groups-language-as-a-barometer)). So the **Status** column matters more than the meaning column. "Unexplained by design" means do not explain it in the next draft either.
