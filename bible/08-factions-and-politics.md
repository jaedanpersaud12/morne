# 08. Factions and politics

Factions form around **ideas**, never around types of people. Each one is an ecosystem: fighters, healers, builders, cooks, powered and powerless, all mixed. A healthy forest needs decomposers, pollinators, predators and shade plants, and so does a faction. The drama comes from people in the wrong faction.

## The four

| Faction | Belief | Has | Lacks | Drift over time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Compound | Protect what we have and hold until rescue | Walls, medicine, water, a generator, a medic | Food, numbers, fuel | From neighbourhood to fort; from "protect" to "keep out" |
| The Harbour | Order through strength | Fish, boats, warehouses, the gas station, the hospital stores, the most kids | Medicine know-how, clean water, trust | From the island's only government to a food-controlled regime |
| The Hills | The island isn't the enemy | Crops, land knowledge, the truth about the network | Defences, medicine | From quiet farmers to something like a faith |
| The Shore | Every kid for themselves, or get off the island | Salvage, rumours from the ships | Everything else | Small, unpredictable, the island's unreliable newspaper |

## The fifth group: nobody

About a hundred kids by month six who won't join anyone: crews of three to eight, raiders, kids with ridiculous powers and nothing to do with them. They are not a faction. They are the island's weather. The Junction crew below the hill, the Mas Camp on the coast road, the Gas Truck, the Lighthouse. Full list in [07](07-powers-in-practice.md#the-unaligned-crews-raiders-and-kids-with-ridiculous-powers).

## Firepower

| | Powered | Fighters | Guns | What that means |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Compound | 8 | 4 | 1 | Can't win a fight. Can refuse to have one: a hill, a wall, a welded gate, the twins. |
| Harbour | about 120 | about 30 | 20 to 25 | Can take anything on the island by force except the hill, cheaply. |
| Hills | about 130 | a handful | a few | Won't fight. Roots what dies there. |
| Crews | about 15 | all of them | 3 or 4 | Can't hold anything. Can take anything that isn't guarded tonight. |

The Compound's politics follow from that table: it survives by being worth more alive (Imani's hands, Tori's ice) than it would cost to take. See [07](07-powers-in-practice.md#combat).

## The three questions every faction splits on

1. **Wait or build?** Is rescue coming, or is nobody coming and we need a real society?
2. **Who leads?** Should powered kids rule because they can protect everyone, or is that the start of a hierarchy?
3. **Medicine or the island?** Keep sleepers alive with dwindling supplies, or give them to the earth?

## Resources are the real politics

See [13](13-resources-and-roles.md) for the full ledger. The short version:

- **Food.** Powers burn calories, so whoever controls food controls who can fight. The Harbour holds the fish. The Hills hold the crops. The Compound holds neither, which is its weakness.
- **Fuel.** The Harbour holds the gas station and the fishing fleet's diesel. Without fuel, no generators, no boats, no trucks. The Compound's generators die in weeks without a deal.
- **Ice.** [Tori](cast/tori.md) can preserve food and medicine. Every faction wants her.
- **Medicine.** The Compound's cupboard. The hospital in town, which the Harbour holds and nobody knows how to use.
- **Fresh water.** The Compound's tanks and pools versus the Harbour's dead mains and the Hills' streams.
- **Information.** [Kai](cast/kai.md)'s radio. He knows what the outside world is saying before anyone else, and he's the only line to Judge.

## How power shifts over three years

1. **Year one: scramble.** Every group hoards. The Compound closes its gates. [Judge](cast/judge.md) organises food when nobody else will and becomes the closest thing to government. The children's house at the fish market is the first thing he's admired for, and his first lever.
2. **Year two: trade and pressure.** The Compound's medicine runs low and it must trade with the Harbour for fish and fuel. The Hills' rooted sleepers survive while others die, and kids start going north. The Harbour starts taxing. Judge and [the Gardener](cast/gardener.md) make their deal: the strongman and the saint.
3. **Year three: the question of the sleepers.** Something changes in the rooted adults. The Hills know first. The island's politics stop being about food and become about what the sleepers are becoming, and who gets to decide.

## How small things move the world

Write these as ripples, not events. The protagonists see smoke, a price, a face at the gate; they rarely see the cause.

- A generator running all night on the hill tells the whole island who has fuel. (Night one.)
- One box of antibiotics traded to the Harbour sets the price of medicine for a year.
- Tori freezing a fish hold for Judge makes her the most valuable person on the island and puts a target on the Compound.
- A rooted sleeper living when a bedridden one dies empties a faction.
- A boat sunk at the quarantine line ends the "rescue is coming" faction overnight.
- The day the Compound turns away a five-year-old is the day it stops being the good guys to everyone outside.

## Leadership inside the Compound

**Nobody leads.** That is the point, and the theme. The Compound runs on domains, each held by the friend who can hold it:

| Domain | Who | Why it's theirs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The sleepers and medicine | [Imani](cast/imani.md) | The books, the table in her head |
| The gate, the wall, the runs outside | [Danny](cast/danny.md) and [Lonny](cast/lonny.md) | Heat and muscle; they are the ones who go out |
| Power, radio, Judge | [Kai](cast/kai.md) | The generator, the handheld, the only line to town |
| The little ones and morale | [Asha](cast/asha.md) | Loud, patient, impossible to ignore |
| Food and cold | [Tori](cast/tori.md) | Ice turns a day's fish into a week's food |
| The ground, the Hills, Ezra | [Nia](cast/nia.md) | The only one who hears it |

Decisions that cross domains get made in the Clarkes' kitchen, by the six, arguing. The goat is how they argue.

**The pressure from outside is that someone must be in charge.** Judge wants a name to deal with. The kids at the gate ask for the doctor. Troy and the older boys ask why one friend group decides for forty kids, and the honest answer is that nobody else was in the kitchen on night one. Everyone lands on Imani because she was in the paper. She keeps trying to carry it. The friends keep taking it off her. That tension is Imani's arc and the Compound's politics at the same time.

**Troy's complaint is sharper than "why her":** *why them?* Six friends who were a group before the Sleep now run a compound of forty, and the other thirty-four never chose it. He is right about that, which is why the Gardener gets to him first.
