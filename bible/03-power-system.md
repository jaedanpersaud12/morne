# 03. The power system

The element is the alphabet; the manifestation is the handwriting. Everyone with the same element shares a broad shape, but nobody's power looks the same, and you can read a person from how their power moves.

## Two things shape a power

1. **What marked you decides the element.** The broad type of experience.
2. **Who you are decides the manifestation.** The power answers one question: *what did this person's body learn to do to survive?* It is shaped like the wound and the person. Never a lookup table.

### The backbone, held loosely

The four stress responses (fight, flight, freeze, fawn) colour how a power leans. A tendency, not a rule, and it can shift over the book. A shift in manifestation after a new trauma is how corruption or growth becomes visible on the page.

### Not every trait needs a wound

Wounds shape powers. Personality is just who someone is. Some people are funny because they are funny.

## The six elements

Each one is a real body system pushed past its limit. That is what keeps it grounded.

| Element | Body system | Typical mark | Cost of overuse | Who has it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fire | Metabolism: burning fat and sugar as fuel | Loss, anger, shame | Starvation, fevers, wasting | [Danny](cast/danny.md) |
| Water | Fluid pressure: moisture from body and air | Grief, adapting, hiding | Dehydration, kidney failure | [Tori](cast/tori.md) (ice), [Judge](cast/judge.md) (the sea) |

| Lightning | Nervous system (the body already runs on electricity) | Sudden shock | Nerve damage, seizures | Dev |
| Life / decay | Cell growth and death. The rarest. The circle of life as an element. | Witnessing death, caretaking, the edge | Your own lifespan | [Nia](cast/nia.md), Ezra, [the Gardener](cast/gardener.md) (all six, faintly), [Imani](cast/imani.md) at the age line |

## The one rule that makes it grounded

**Conservation of energy.** Nothing comes from nothing. Every flame is paid for in calories, body heat, or something else's life. This is physics, it is "every gift takes something," and it is the circle of life in one rule.

Practical consequences the book must honour:
- A powered kid eats two to three times what a normal kid eats. See [13](13-resources-and-roles.md#food).
- A big use leaves a kid shaking, cold, and starving. Danny after welding a gate cannot stand for an hour.
- Nobody can keep it up. Powers are for moments, not for living.

## Where it comes from

The fungal network reads stress hormones (adrenaline, cortisol) and rewires its host. Real epigenetics: experiences switch genes on and off. "Your nature is molded by your experiences" is literal biology here. See [05](05-network-and-gardener.md).

## How many, and where

One in ten of the island's 10-to-19s by the end of year one. Quarry Trace runs at nearly one in three because it sits on the network's quarry wound. The Harbour has fifteen times the Trace's powered population in absolute terms. Full numbers, daily uses, combat rules and the unaligned crews are in [07](07-powers-in-practice.md).

## How powers arrive

Like puberty. Uneven, scary, embarrassing, uncontrolled. Some kids early, some late, some never. Nobody chose it and nobody knows what is normal.

- **First signs are small and deniable.** A glass of water that comes out cold. A boy who doesn't bruise. A girl who stops a fan with her breath and doesn't notice.
- **Onset over weeks.** The first obvious use is usually an accident under stress.
- **Control takes months** and is never total.
- **Awakening order in the Trace:** Tori (ice, night one, deniable), Danny (heat, week one), Lonny (strength, week one to two), Nia (she already heard the ground on night one; plants by week two), Kai and Asha (month two to three, and nobody expects the dance), Troy (Earth, month three), the Lall girl (Water, finds water underground, month five).

## Rules we can use later

- **Food is power.** Powers burn calories. Whoever controls food controls who can fight.
- **Powers mutate.** New trauma can shift a manifestation. A healer who starts fighting may find her gift turning to rot.
- **Going to seed.** Push too far and the network takes you completely. You become part of the island. The Gardener is the one who survived it.
- **Echoes.** A dead kid's power can resurface in someone else, carrying fragments of memory. The person who inherits the power of someone the group lost, or someone the group killed.
- **Real chemistry combos.** Lightning splits water into hydrogen and oxygen; add fire and it explodes. Ice colliding in clouds builds charge, so Tori's ice strengthens a storm. Teamwork has physical rules.
- **Linked powers.** Twins or very close pairs can share a loop (see [Kai](cast/kai.md) and [Asha](cast/asha.md)). Rare, and the network seems to like it.

## What a power says about a person (manifestation examples)

These are illustrations of the rule, not canon unless a cast file says so.

- A girl whose father didn't come back from the sea lay awake holding her breath for months. Water. She doesn't move water; she **stills** it. Later she learns blood is water too.
- The middle kid nobody noticed, who kept peace by being useful and invisible. Fire. **Invisible fire**: no flame, just heat. Rooms warm when he's there. He can cook something from the inside and nobody would suspect him.
- A girl trapped under rubble for three days. Earth. She doesn't throw rocks. **She becomes immovable.** The more afraid, the heavier. In a full panic she can't move either.
- A boy talked over his whole life. Air, in **his voice**. A shout hits like a pressure wave. Whispering does nothing. It only works when he's furious, which is a problem.
