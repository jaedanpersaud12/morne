## Three movements, if it becomes a trilogy

1. **Survive.** The night through the first year. Keeping sleepers alive, the gate, the first powers, the Harbour rising. Ends with the Trace a fort and the Gardener at the gate.
2. **Build.** Year two. Trade, siege, hurricane, the age line, the rooted changing. Ends with the Harbour and the Trace at war, and the Hills holding the truth.
3. **Become.** Year three. The second bloom, the outside world, the sleepers' fate, who the friends have become. Ends by answering the theme's question for each of them. Imani's power (Life/decay at the age line, her lifespan as the cost) is the hinge between books one and two.
