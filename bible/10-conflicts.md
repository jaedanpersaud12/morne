Every fault line inside the friend group maps onto a bigger island-wide question, so when the friends fight, the whole world's conflict plays out in miniature.

## With the island

- **The gate question.** Open it to starving kids from town, or hold it. The Compound's first and defining choice, and it recurs every time supplies change.
- **Trade with the Harbour.** Medicine for fish. Then Tori for fish. Then loyalty for fish. Then fuel.
- **The Harbour comes for the pharmacy.** Judge decides medicine should be shared "fairly," meaning under his control. Siege, negotiation, or betrayal from inside.
- **Kids leaving for the Hills.** When rooted sleepers survive and bedridden ones die, Compound families start carrying their parents north. The Compound empties from inside.
- **The little ones.** Whose job is it to feed forty children under ten? Everyone's, and so nobody's. See [12](12-little-ones.md).
- **Shak's stories.** A rumour that the navy is taking kids off the island. True? The Shore believes it. Someone will try.

## With the crews

- **The road.** The Junction crew below the hill makes the twelve houses a place you cross fast. The gate exists because of them. Month two they try it at night, and Danny leaves a handprint on a boy's arm the whole road hears about.
- **The water run.** Dry season, the Hills road, two drums of water, and the Mas Camp. Asha's wrist. Lonny carrying her home instead of the water.
- **Rust.** The younger Mohammed brother can open any lock and kill any engine. Every faction wants him. He wants none of them. Whoever he ends up with changes the balance.
- **Sleepy.** A girl whose touch looks like the Sleep. The crews' nightmare, and the Compound's, because the first time she touches someone from the hill, everyone thinks the Sleep came for a kid.
- **The siege.** Month nine or ten, the Harbour comes for the pharmacy with guns and thirty dock boys. All eight powers, one shotgun, the wall. The Compound wins and somebody dies. See [07](07-powers-in-practice.md#the-four-fights-of-book-one).

## With the outside world

- **The navy's silence.** They watch, they drop supplies that stop coming, they never land. What are they waiting for?
- **The voice on the radio.** Kai makes contact. The outside world wants samples, data, a rooted sleeper. What does Kai give them?
- **A boat gets too close.** What the navy does to it. The kids learn they are not being protected; they are being contained.
- **Someone from outside comes in anyway.** An adult who lands and falls asleep on the beach. A scientist in a suit. Who gets to them first?

## With the network

- **The rooted change.** Hair, skin, breath. The Hills kids say the sleepers are dreaming. Are they?
- **Going to seed.** The first powered kid who pushes too far and becomes part of the island. The group watches it happen to someone they know.
- **Echoes.** A dead kid's power turning up in a living one, with fragments of memory.
- **The bloom returns.** A second spore season. Everyone borderline rolls the dice again. Imani's birthday lands in it.

## Moral questions with no clean answer

- Do you keep feeding a sleeper when a living child needs the water?
- Is a Harbour strongman who feeds hundreds worse than a Compound that feeds forty well?
- If rooting works, is refusing it (Farah) principle or murder?
- When Judge offers protection in exchange for Tori, what does Imani say?
- If the outside world offers to take one kid off the island, who goes?
- Is Miss Yvonne, who is nobody's family, owed the same water as Granny?
