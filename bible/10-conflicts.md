## With the island

- **The door.** The Trace takes in the sick, always, and builds so it can take in more. The limit comes when one more would kill someone inside. It recurs every time supplies change, and [Adrian](cast/adrian.md) makes it dangerous. Full design in [08](08-factions-and-politics.md#the-door-who-the-trace-takes-in-and-where-it-stops).
- **Adrian Castell.** The quarry owner's son. Taken in hurt in month 3, claims the place in month 5, put out with a third of the medicine, and sits below the bend for the rest of the book. The plain bad person: he wants what the Trace built, not what it has. See [cast/adrian](cast/adrian.md).
- **Trade with the Harbour.** Medicine for fish. Then Sol for fish. Then loyalty for fish. Then fuel.
- **The Harbour comes for the pharmacy.** Judge decides medicine should be shared "fairly," meaning under his control. Siege, negotiation, or betrayal from inside.
- **Kids leaving for the Hills.** When rooted sleepers survive and bedridden ones die, Trace families start carrying their parents north. The Trace empties from inside.
- **The little ones.** Whose job is it to feed every little one who comes up the hill? Everyone's, and so nobody's. See [12](12-little-ones.md).
- **Zu's stories.** A rumour that the navy is taking kids off the island. True? The Shore believes it. Someone will try.

## With the crews

- **The road.** The Junction crew below the hill makes the twelve houses a place you cross fast. The barricade exists because of them. Month two they come up it at night, and Tock leaves a handprint on a boy's arm the whole road hears about.
- **The water run.** Dry season, the Hills road, two drums of water, and the Mas Camp. Lyra's wrist. Lonny carrying her home instead of the water.
- **Rust.** The younger Harker brother can open any lock and kill any engine. Every faction wants him. He wants none of them. Whoever he ends up with changes the balance.
- **Sleepy.** A girl whose touch looks like the Sleep. The crews' nightmare, and the Trace's, because the first time she touches someone from the hill, everyone thinks the Sleep came for a kid.
- **The siege.** Month nine or ten, the Harbour comes for the pharmacy with guns and thirty dock boys. All eight powers, one shotgun, the wall. The Trace wins and somebody dies. See [07](07-powers-in-practice.md#the-four-fights-of-book-one).

## With the outside world

- **The navy's silence.** They watch, they drop supplies that stop coming, they never land. What are they waiting for?
- **The voice on the radio.** Fen makes contact. The outside world wants samples, data, a rooted sleeper. What does Fen give them?
- **A boat gets too close.** What the navy does to it. The kids learn they are not being protected; they are being contained.
- **Someone from outside comes in anyway.** An adult who lands and falls asleep on the beach. A scientist in a suit. Who gets to them first?

## With the network

- **The rooted change.** Hair, skin, breath. The Hills kids say the sleepers are dreaming. Are they?
- **Going to seed.** The first powered kid who pushes too far and becomes part of the island. The group watches it happen to someone they know.
- **Echoes.** A dead kid's power turning up in a living one, with fragments of memory.
- **The bloom returns.** A second spore season. Everyone borderline rolls the dice again. Juno's birthday lands in it.

## Moral questions with no clean answer

- Do you keep feeding a sleeper when a living child needs the water?
- Is a Harbour strongman who feeds hundreds worse than a Trace that feeds thirty well?
- If rooting works, is refusing it (Ida) principle or murder?
- When Judge offers protection in exchange for Sol, what does Juno say?
- If the outside world offers to take one kid off the island, who goes?
- Is Miss Tilda, who is nobody's family, owed the same water as Granny?
- How many healthy kids do you turn away to keep one sick one alive? And if you took the wrong one in once, do you check the next sick girl at the gate?
