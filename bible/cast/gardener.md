# The Gardener

**Antagonist: the island. Element: all six, faintly. Name: open.**

## Who he was

A Hills boy, fifteen, who awakened in the first week with Life/decay, pushed it too far trying to save his grandmother, and went to seed. The network took him. He did not die. He is fully rooted and still walking.

## Who he is now

Calm, gentle, absolutely sincere. Bare feet, always; he can't bear shoes between him and the ground. Fine pale threads at the hairline and wrists that people take for scars. He speaks with the voices of the island's dead when he wants to, and his own voice, which is soft, when he doesn't.

## What he wants

**Nobody to lose anyone ever again.** When he went into the ground he got his family back. He hears his rooted grandmother and his dead little sister. From his side, death is solved. He looks at the Compound's thirty-one sleepers wasting in beds and sees cruelty. He looks at grieving kids and sees a problem he can fix.

## What he does about it

1. **Every sleeper into the earth.** He walks the island persuading kids to carry their parents to the ground. He never forces it. He never has to.
2. **Every kid into the earth, eventually.** Rooting while alive, like him. He calls it coming home. The friends call it going to seed.
3. **The seeds off the island.** The network needs carriers through the quarantine. He wants the strongest powered kids on a boat, and he will tell them it is rescue.

## How he antagonises

He never raises a hand. He is the grey one; the people are the "bad" ones. Full mechanics in [05](../05-network-and-gardener.md#the-gardener). In short: he is always right, people convert and the converts enforce, and the network's pull does the rest.

**His bargain:** *The island remembers everyone. Nobody has to die.* He can let a kid hear their sleeping mother's voice. It works, because it is partly true.

## Why he is the theme's mirror

The friends say *we are what survives the fire.* The Gardener says *nothing has to burn; come into the ground and keep everything.* Rebirth without loss. He is what happens when rebirth wins and the self loses. He thinks he is saving everyone; the network, through him, is making sure it survives. He can no longer tell the difference.

## What he wants from the friends

[Nia](nia.md), because she can already hear it. [Imani](imani.md), because she is the one keeping the sleepers in beds. Late in the story he offers Imani Granny's voice. All she has to do is sit in the garden and put her hands in the soil.

## In book one

A rumour from the Hills. Then a name. Then a visitor at the gate, barefoot, asking for Nia by name, who has never met him.
