# Kit Wick, 15

**POV character. Element: Life / decay. Depth tier 2.**

## Who she is

The taxi driver's daughter. She and her father moved up the Trace eight months ago, into a rented two-room flat at the bend, the first house on the road. On the Trace, never really of it. Sharp-tongued, streetwise, a town girl with family in Morne Bay. Eight months of hearing the group come down the hill as a noise, and nobody ever told her what the goat was. It wasn't meanness. A group that has been together since before they could talk doesn't notice who is outside it. Juno noticed. Juno always noticed.

Half in the group because of [Fen](fen.md), and the nights in the parked taxi with the radio on, which neither of them has told anyone about.

## Inside

**Want:** To keep her father alive, with her own hands, at the bend. On night one she keeps him awake for hours and then gets him on his side ([ch 1](../../chapters/ch01-dust/LATEST.md)); when Lonny carries every other sleeper into the Mosses' front room, she keeps Ambrose on the gallery and says nothing ([ch 5](../../chapters/ch05-spoon/LATEST.md), line 7). In ch 9 the want becomes the act: she roots him in the yard, alone, at night, without asking ([17](../17-book-one-outline.md#movement-2-the-scramble-ch-511-weeks-16)).

**Need:** To stop standing on the step: to be inside something without first proving she can manage alone. "Being asked wasn't the same as being there" ([ch 1](../../chapters/ch01-dust/LATEST.md), line 63); the need is to find out what being there costs, and pay it.

**Lie:** That she is outside every group by default, so anything that matters she has to do by herself and tell no one. It is why she says no to the beach every time, and why the rooting happens without asking.

**Fear:** Being left. Her mother left and her father has fallen, and the one number she knows by heart rang out. She doesn't let herself think about whether that was the dust or just her mother.

**Wound:** Her mother leaving last year and staying in town, and her father's face when Kit asked why. She stopped asking. The move up the hill, somewhere cheaper with a yard for the car, is the wound's address: she has been the new girl ever since.

**How she loves:** By keeping watch and doing the job. She sits on the step where she can see him through the open car door and doesn't take her eyes off him; she makes him walk to the Pells' gate and back; she drags a mattress into the shade and sits beside it with her knees up ([ch 1](../../chapters/ch01-dust/LATEST.md), [ch 4](../../chapters/ch04-ice/LATEST.md)). With Fen it is the same: things done, never explained ([09](../09-relationships.md#fen-and-kit-the-taxi)).

**How it shows:** Watching from the step or the gallery, with something in her hand (a clothes peg). Lifting a hand when the group goes past, without being asked. Fear turns into counting and lists she doesn't know she is making: "Twelve." She hadn't known she'd counted ([ch 1](../../chapters/ch01-dust/LATEST.md), line 153); running down the hill making a list of what else she should have done. She reads faces before words (Juno's "her too" was not fear, it was a girl adding something up).

**Humour:** Dry, and aimed at whoever is pretending. "It's November. Dust comes in June." "You said that last time." She deflates; she doesn't perform.

**What breaks her:** Being offered her father back. In ch 28 the Gardener stands at the gate, asks for Kit by name, and offers her Ambrose's voice ([17](../17-book-one-outline.md#movement-5-the-garden-ch-2830-months-1012)). And the whispers, which don't stop, some of which know her name.

**Happy:** A Sunday morning before the dust: the washing on the line, the car washed before the sun gets hot, her father in his pressed shirt with the service on the radio. In ch 1, when he does ten squats in the yard to stay awake, she laughs too, and for a minute it is almost fine. The radio in the parked taxi at night with Fen.

**Secret:** The nights in the taxi with Fen. That she dialled her mother on night one, the first time she had ever used the number, and nobody answered. The key to the shotgun on a cord round her neck, which Judge and now Juno know about, and she doesn't know they know ([ch 8](../../chapters/ch08-market/LATEST.md), [19](../19-imagery-and-threads.md#secrets-ledger-who-knows-what-by-chapter-8)). That the whispers comfort her (**PROPOSED**, see below).

**Book one leaves her:** Inside the group, and the cost of that is the whole story. She has rooted her father and broken with Juno over it (ch 9 to 10), fired the Trace's only gun once (siege, ch 25), held the hill on siege night two, her POV, when somebody dies (ch 26; Bix is the outline's proposal), been asked for by name by the Gardener (ch 28), and found Juno and Tock carrying Granny to the garden at night (ch 29), the act that mends them. Ambrose's eyes are open by movement 5 and track nothing; the Gardener says he is dreaming of her. Her mother is still in town, a book-two thread.

## Emotional palette

**Shows most:** defiance.
**Hides:** rage at her mother for leaving.
**The surprise:** the whispers of the dead comfort her, and that scares her more than fear would. (Approved; **PROPOSED** on the page until a chapter shows the whispers.)

She speaks in short reports and flat corrections: a fact, a number, a place. Lighter dialect than her father ("Dust don't read calendar," "I not sick. Is the dust"), heavier than Juno, with town in it ("There's people"). Her narration thinks the way Ambrose would have, in steps: on his side, something under his head, then help.

## Family

Her father, Ambrose Wick, has driven a taxi for sixteen years: shirt pressed every morning even on a Sunday, crease ironed sharp, the car washed before the sun is hot, a tally book of fares in neat capitals, soft voice people lean in to hear. On Sundays he sits in the car with the church service on the radio, because the car is the only thing he owns outright and a man should sit in what he owns ([ch 1](../../chapters/ch01-dust/LATEST.md)). He is the last adult on the Trace to fall, at a quarter to five, because she kept him on his feet.

Her mother left last year and stayed in town; that is why they moved up the hill, somewhere cheaper with a yard for the car. Kit stopped asking why inside a week, because of what her father's face did. She has the number from a birthday card. On night one she dials it for the first time, and it rings out in Morne Bay, and she doesn't let herself think about whether that's the dust or just her mother. **Status unknown**, and a thread for later: her mother is in town, which means she is in Judge's town. In movement 3 Nell mentions a woman asleep in a flat on the coast road with Kit's surname on the mailbox; Kit doesn't go. Book two ([17](../17-book-one-outline.md#side-arcs-under-the-main-plot)).

## The groove

Always "the new girl", "the taxi man's daughter." Welcome, never belonging. Not because they shut her out: they tried (Lyra's plate of pelau, Lonny's wave, Sol's charger, the goat explained twice and lost in the shouting, [ch 1](../../chapters/ch01-dust/LATEST.md)). Every joke has a footnote, and the footnote is a childhood she wasn't in. Half of it is hers too: she says no to the beach every time. Watching from the step at the bend. From night one the step becomes a post: Juno tells her nobody comes past the bend, and the bible makes it the guard's seat ("the guard is whoever sits on the Wick gallery," [06](../06-island.md)).

## Power

The rarest element. Plants lean toward her, scraps rot into compost overnight, she can feel living things nearby, and she can hear the network, like whispers under the ground. She heard it first on night one, standing in the road with the corbeaux on the taxi, and didn't know what it was. **PROPOSED for ch 1:** one deniable line at the corbeaux (a sound under the road she takes for the dust, or her own blood), never named; until that line is written, the first sign on the page is ch 9. She is the Trace's bridge to the Hills and to Moth. See [03](../03-power-system.md) and [05](../05-network-and-gardener.md). In ch 9 she thinks the whispers are grief ([17](../17-book-one-outline.md#movement-2-the-scramble-ch-511-weeks-16)).

**Cost:** the whispers don't stop. They are the island's dead, and some of them know her name.

**The surprise (approved, PROPOSED on the page):** the whispers comfort her. The dead are company on a gallery where she has sat alone since March, and that she finds them a comfort scares her more than fear would.

## Role

She roots her father in the yard at the bend, the first on the Trace to do it, and it works. That one act splits the group: Juno's medicine versus Kit's island. The kid who never belonged is the one the island chose. See [10](../10-conflicts.md#inside-the-group).

## As a lens

She opens the book. The outsider watching this loud, close group laugh at things nobody else understands, so the reader learns their jokes alongside her. By the end, the reader is in the group, and so is she, and the cost of that is the whole story. See [14](../14-writing-rules.md#the-groups-private-language).

## Relationships

- Ambrose: everything. She is the one who keeps him: awake on night one, on his side in the shade, on the gallery instead of in the ward. "Worse than her mother," he tells her, and then says sorry twice ([ch 1](../../chapters/ch01-dust/LATEST.md)). The feeling is devotion with no one to share the weight. In book one she roots him (ch 9), and every Kit chapter after checks on him in one line; by movement 5 his eyes are open and track nothing ([17](../17-book-one-outline.md#side-arcs-under-the-main-plot)).
- [Fen](fen.md): the taxi at night. On the page, a look at the ground beside her left foot and a joke he still hasn't explained ([ch 1](../../chapters/ch01-dust/LATEST.md)). He is the reason she is half in the group; she is part of why he is still alive, and he has never said so. On night one he goes to the roof instead of to her. In month one he is between her and Juno and chooses neither out loud, and she notices. When the twins awaken (ch 16) the secret stops being keepable; by the end of year one the Gardener asks for her and Fen wants the gate shut, the first time they want opposite things. They never explain their feelings in a scene ([09](../09-relationships.md#fen-and-kit-the-taxi)).
- [Juno](juno.md): the one who never stopped asking. The argument. They all tried with Kit; Juno is the one who kept on asking, every time. Kit runs to the Vales' on night one because of it ([ch 1](../../chapters/ch01-dust/LATEST.md)), and Juno answers her with "Good. Good, Kit." They count the same way. Then Kit roots Ambrose without asking and it makes Juno's medicine look like cruelty; neither is wrong, they stop speaking, Fen carries messages. It mends with an act: Kit finds Juno at the garden at night (ch 29) ([09](../09-relationships.md#juno-and-kit-the-argument)).
- Her mother: hidden rage (see palette). A number known by heart and never used until it rang out.
- [Judge](judge.md): has never met her and knows about the key. "You have one, up there. Ambrose's. Kit Wick has the key... I'm not coming for it" ([ch 8](../../chapters/ch08-market/LATEST.md)). Her mother may be in his town.
- Moth ([supporting](supporting.md#the-hills)): the only other person who hears it. In ch 9 he is at the junction, barefoot, telling her the ground will take him; from then on her counterpart and sometimes her guide.
- [The Gardener](gardener.md): wants her most. She can already hear. In ch 28 he asks for her by name and offers her father's voice.

## In a fight, and on a Tuesday

Rot, if she touches and means it, and she hates it. She wears the key to Ambrose's shotgun on a cord (the steel box under his bed), the Trace's only gun, and fires it once in book one. The gun is licensed, bought after a robbery on a night fare, with 11 shells ([06](../06-island.md)). Daily: compost overnight, the garden in weeks, the dead rat in the tank found by feel, which sleeper is failing before Juno knows. See [07](../07-powers-in-practice.md).

## Open questions

- **How the comfort first shows.** Option: the whispers are loudest when she sits alone with Ambrose and she sits longer because of it; or she catches herself missing them on a night they are quiet. Either way, whose voices? Option: never her father's, so the Gardener's offer in ch 28 lands as the one thing they haven't given her; or faintly his, which she tells no one.
- **Where the rage at her mother comes out.** Option: at the phone, redialling a number she knows will ring out; or sideways at Juno, who is doing a mother's job for everyone else on the road.
- **Why the gallery and not the ward (ch 5)?** The page shows the choice with no reason. Outline 17 proposes one (the ward is the road's, and she isn't); that is a proposal, not yet approved.
- **Does Kit learn that Juno knows about the key?** Option: in ch 12, when the gun comes out and Juno already knows where it is; or never, and Juno's knowing is one more kept thing between them.
