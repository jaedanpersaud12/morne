# Kit Wick, 15

**POV character. Element: Life / decay. Depth tier 2.**

## Who she is

The taxi driver's daughter. She and her father moved up the Trace eight months ago, into a rented two-room flat at the bend, the first house on the road. On the Trace, never really of it. Sharp-tongued, streetwise, a town girl with family in Morne Bay. Eight months of hearing the group come down the hill as a noise, and nobody ever told her what the goat was. It wasn't meanness. A group that has been together since before they could talk doesn't notice who is outside it. Juno noticed. Juno always noticed.

Half in the group because of [Fen](fen.md), and the nights in the parked taxi with the radio on, which neither of them has told anyone about.

## Family

Her father, Ambrose Wick, has driven a taxi for sixteen years: shirt pressed every morning even on a Sunday, crease ironed sharp, the car washed before the sun is hot, a tally book of fares in neat capitals, soft voice people lean in to hear. He is the last adult on the Trace to fall, at a quarter to five, because she kept him on his feet.

Her mother left last year and stayed in town; that is why they moved up the hill, somewhere cheaper with a yard for the car. Kit stopped asking why at about ten, because of what her father's face did. She has the number from a birthday card. On night one she dials it for the first time, and it rings out in Morne Bay, and she doesn't let herself think about whether that's the dust or just her mother. **Status unknown**, and a thread for later: her mother is in town, which means she is in Judge's town.

## The groove

Always "the new girl", "the taxi man's daughter." Welcome, never belonging. Watching from the step at the bend.

## Power

The rarest element. Plants lean toward her, scraps rot into compost overnight, she can feel living things nearby, and she can hear the network, like whispers under the ground. She heard it first on night one, standing in the road with the corbeaux on the taxi, and didn't know what it was. She is the Trace's bridge to the Hills and to Moth. See [03](../03-power-system.md) and [05](../05-network-and-gardener.md).

**Cost:** the whispers don't stop. They are the island's dead, and some of them know her name.

## Role

She roots her father in the yard at the bend, the first on the Trace to do it, and it works. That one act splits the group: Juno's medicine versus Kit's island. The kid who never belonged is the one the island chose.

## As a lens

She opens the book. The outsider watching this loud, close group laugh at things nobody else understands, so the reader learns their jokes alongside her. By the end, the reader is in the group, and so is she, and the cost of that is the whole story.

## Relationships

- Ambrose: everything.
- [Fen](fen.md): the taxi at night.
- [Juno](juno.md): the only one who said her name first. The argument.
- Moth ([supporting](supporting.md#the-hills)): the only other person who hears it.
- [The Gardener](gardener.md): wants her most. She can already hear.

## In a fight, and on a Tuesday

Rot, if she touches and means it, and she hates it. She wears the key to Ambrose's shotgun on a cord (the steel box under his bed), the Trace's only gun, and fires it once in book one. Daily: compost overnight, the garden in weeks, the dead rat in the tank found by feel, which sleeper is failing before Juno knows. See [07](../07-powers-in-practice.md).
