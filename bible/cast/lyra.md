# Lyra Quill, 16

**Element: Air (Rush), linked with Fen. Depth tier 3.**

## Core fact

The twin who stayed in dance.

## Who she is

Fen's twin. Outgoing, magnetic, knows every song at every fete, always surrounded by people. Does everything at volume. She and Fen clash sometimes and are still each other's person. She keeps the house loud so nobody has to hear the machines.

## History

Their mother put both twins in a dance company as little kids. Lyra stayed and competed; Fen quit at twelve, the day their mother came home from the first surgery ([Fen](fen.md#history)). Then the long illness: cancer, a hospital bed where the dining table used to be, a night nurse on Sundays. Lyra is the one who kept the contacts. On night one she has the oncologist's number "from the thing in July," and messages him, Auntie Prue and the church group ([ch 3](../../chapters/ch03-signal/LATEST.md), line 17).

## Inside

**Want:** For somebody to answer. On night one it is her phone list: "Nobody's answering. Nobody. I messaged everybody." On day 7 it is the ship: "Fen, is it for us?" ([ch 3](../../chapters/ch03-signal/LATEST.md) line 13, [ch 6](../../chapters/ch06-sixteen/LATEST.md) line 107). When nobody comes, she becomes the one who answers: she takes the little ones before Juno can ([17](../17-book-one-outline.md), ch 11; [12](../12-little-ones.md)).

**Need:** To be able to stand the quiet. Stillness is literally Fen's half of the power; alone she can't brake and crashes ([07](../07-powers-in-practice.md)). What she needs from book one is to stop without someone else to stop her.

**Lie:** That if it's loud enough, nothing bad can get in. She kept the house loud through a year of machines so nobody had to hear them.

**Fear:** The quiet. On night one the whole island goes quiet and the machines are the loudest thing in the house; when the grid dies at 12:14 she is the one breathing fast in the dark ([ch 3](../../chapters/ch03-signal/LATEST.md), line 151).

**Wound:** The same year as Fen's, lived the other way: she stayed in the studio and kept the house loud while he went silent and learned the machines. Their mother's hands curled on nothing for months, even with the morphine ([ch 3](../../chapters/ch03-signal/LATEST.md), line 45).

**How they love:** Out loud and with her hands. She straightens the blanket where it doesn't need straightening, puts her hand over her mother's open one and leaves it there, falls asleep across the foot of the bed still reaching for her ([ch 3](../../chapters/ch03-signal/LATEST.md), lines 25, 51, 127). She runs inside to tell her mother about the ship ([ch 6](../../chapters/ch06-sixteen/LATEST.md), line 111). With people, "Lyra being Lyra, with everyone, loudly" ([09](../09-relationships.md#lyra-and-the-convert)).

**How it shows:** Her thumb moving on the phone without stopping. Tidying what is already tidy. Sleep that takes her mid-sentence: she falls asleep on the Vales' stairs at three in the morning before she finishes giving the generator news ([ch 4](../../chapters/ch04-ice/LATEST.md), line 5). When she is frightened she borrows other people's words: "Okay. Okay. Okay," the way Juno says it ([ch 3](../../chapters/ch03-signal/LATEST.md), line 165). When something is very wrong she stops singing mid-song (**PROPOSED** until shown). Linked, Fen feels it.

**Humour:** Quick, warm, and she names things. The Pell baby is "Pell," "like a footballer," because "she's the only Pell who's awake. She's earned it" ([ch 4](../../chapters/ch04-ice/LATEST.md), lines 45-49). At the goat she says "oh my God" into her arms on the stairs. Fen's joke about her is the truest thing said about her: she'll want to try the generator, and she'll flood it.

**What breaks them:** Choosing between Cass and her twin. The choice is not close, and it still breaks her ([09](../09-relationships.md#lyra-and-the-convert)).

**Happy:** At a fete, knowing every song, surrounded by people, doing everything at volume. In the Trace after the Sleep: loud, patient and impossible to ignore with a four-year-old, which is exactly what a four-year-old needs.

**Secret:** None of her own on the page yet. The secrets around her are Fen's: that their mother's sats hold at 94 on room air, which he keeps from her until (**PROPOSED**) the garden in ch 18 ([19](../19-imagery-and-threads.md#secrets-ledger-who-knows-what-by-chapter-8)), and what the navy said when she asked if the ship was for them. Whether she has one is an open question.

She and Fen carry their mother to the garden (ch 18). Cass converts and carries sleepers out; she finds out through Fen and chooses her twin (ch 23). On the wall in the siege (ch 25). ## Power

Rush. She adds force: huge leaps, acceleration, flinging things. Alone she is fast and acrobatic but overshoots; she can't brake, so she crashes and breaks things. She dumps her momentum into Fen to stop, turn, or freeze mid-air. Arrives month two to three with Fen's, and nobody expects the dance ([03](../03-power-system.md)).

### The twins' power: flow

Two koi circling in a pond. Each swims fine alone; together they make a circle that never stops moving. The element is Air, split in two, and the science is conservation of momentum: energy is never lost, only passed along, the way a skater spins faster pulling her arms in, or aikido turns an attacker's force against them.

| | Fen: Stillness | Lyra: Rush |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does | Absorbs force | Adds force |
| Alone | Can't release fast; fills up and it hurts | Can't stop; crashes |
| Passes | Stored force, for her strikes and leaps | Excess momentum, which he stores as anchor power |

Together, every hit aimed at one is absorbed and passed to the other, who releases it. They circle each other and the opponent like a dance. The harder you attack them, the stronger they get. Like partnering: the flyer's momentum lets the base throw; the base lets the flyer land.

**Costs:** when they're apart too long, Fen fills up with nowhere to put it and Lyra gets reckless. When they're linked they feel each other's mood. A normal sibling fight is dangerous.

## In a fight, and on a Tuesday

Fast, high, hits from above, hard to hit, can't stop. Daily: strings wire between roofs, up the water tank in a jump, the road to the junction in four minutes with a message. Month four the ambush on the Hills road puts her through a fence and breaks her wrist. See [07](../07-powers-in-practice.md). Costs day to day: scrapes, sprains; she can't brake without Fen.
