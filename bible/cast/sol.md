# Sol Finch, 16

**Element: Water (ice). Depth tier 3.**

## Core facts

Her family runs the ice plant for the fishermen. She is gay, and out to no one.

## Who she is

Autistic, happy, an only child. Loves football and watches every match she can; she doesn't play. Round and solid. Holds her phone in both hands when she's scared. Turned the TV off when she found her parents on the couch, and turned it back on because the quiet was worse.

She was already in the Trace before the six noticed her; she lived two doors from Lonny and the group absorbed her somewhere around age nine. She is the one who laughs first at the goat.

## Core pillar: being seen

She is gay in a place where coming out can cost you your family and your friends. The fear is not abstract. It is the fear of losing the exact people you love most, which is why she has never told them. Where she is at the start of the book (out to nobody, or to one person) is still [open](../16-open-questions.md).

Her story asks the theme's question most personally: when the old world's rules are gone, does the new one grow back better, or rebuild the same prejudices? Who accepts her, who doesn't, and whether the Trace becomes somewhere she can be herself. A crush on Lux, a girl from the Harbour whose family worked the ice plant, ties her to the other side.

Writing rule: let the reader sit in the risk with her before anyone's answer comes. Acceptance from the group should cost something and mean something, not arrive for free.

## The groove

The cold room at the ice plant was her quiet place when the world got too loud. She has spent years keeping part of herself frozen, preserved but hidden. The power is shaped like that.

## Power

She makes ice on a tropical island, best when she is in her rhythm, worse in chaos. The first sign was a sweating glass of water on night one that nobody but Juno noticed. Ice preserves food, breaks fevers and keeps medicine cold: one of the most valuable gifts in the Trace, and the reason every faction wants her. See [13](../13-resources-and-roles.md#food).

**Cost:** dehydration; she drinks constantly. And the pull: by year two she feels the network's homesickness for the sea more than anyone. See [05](../05-network-and-gardener.md#what-it-wants).

## Relationships

- [Juno](juno.md): the one who noticed the ice and said nothing. Sol trusts her for that.
- [Lonny](lonny.md): neighbours. He treats her like a little sister, which she half likes.
- Lux ([supporting](supporting.md#the-harbour)): the crush. The Harbour. Where Lux stands on Sol being gay is one of the quiet, high-stakes questions of the book.

## In a fight, and on a Tuesday

She won't, for a long time. When she finally does (the siege), wet ground freezes under the dock boys' feet and they fall. Daily is where she matters: the fish hold, the medicine box, fevers broken with a hand on the forehead, ice for trade, the pool kept from going bad. Her hands are always cold now. See [07](../07-powers-in-practice.md).
