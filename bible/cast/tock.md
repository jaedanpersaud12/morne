# Tock Vale, 16

**Element: Fire. Depth tier 3.**

## Core fact

Granny called his anger sinful, something to pray out of him. He half believes it, which makes it come out worse.

## Who he is

Juno's younger brother. Built like a fire hydrant, buzz cut, a white nick through his left eyebrow from a fete at fourteen. Hot-headed, loose, fights anyone who disrespects his people, and the first to have your back. He comes to everything that scares him angry. When he goes still, that is the worst sign.

He was the one who walked past the Pells' crib on night one, and the one who went back.

## Power

He cannot throw fire. Heat builds inside him with emotion. Angry, it explodes outward: a hand on a doorframe leaves a scorch. Calm, he can focus it into his hands, hot enough to cut and weld metal. The boy whose anger was called sinful becomes the one who builds the walls that keep everyone safe. See [03](../03-power-system.md).

**Cost:** fevers, dehydration, hunger, and real fear of hurting someone he loves. After welding a gate he can't stand for an hour.

## Fault line

Tock vs. himself. Is his power a sin or a gift? Who does he hurt first, and is it on purpose?

## Relationships

- [Juno](juno.md): she can talk him down. Nobody else can.
- [Lonny](lonny.md): his partner on every run. The muscle and the heat.
- [Olli](olli.md): Tock is the fun one. Olli copies his walk.
- The Pell baby: he carried her for twenty minutes and wouldn't give her over. She is his, in a way nobody says out loud.

## Open

- Whether he ever hits someone with the heat on purpose.
- What Granny says to him if she wakes.

## In a fight

A hand on skin burns. A hand on a cutlass makes the other kid drop it. The gate glows and nobody touches it. Angry, he burns people he didn't mean to; calm, he's slow. Month two he leaves a handprint on a Junction boy's arm and doesn't speak for two days. Daily, he welds, cuts, boils, sterilises. See [07](../07-powers-in-practice.md).
