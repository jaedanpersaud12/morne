# Nia Pierre, 15

**POV character. Element: Life / decay. Depth tier 2.**

## Who she is

The security guard's daughter, raised in two concrete rooms behind the gatehouse that the compound calls the staff annex. Inside the Compound, never really of it. Sharp-tongued, streetwise, with family in Morne Bay. Fifteen years on the road and nobody ever told her what the goat was. It wasn't meanness. A group that old doesn't notice who is outside it. Imani noticed. Imani always noticed.

Half in the group because of [Kai](kai.md), and the gatehouse nights neither of them has told anyone about.

## Family

Her father, Winston Pierre, has worked the gate sixteen years: pale blue shirt pressed every morning, crease ironed sharp, stands in the booth like he's holding it up, soft voice people lean in to hear. He is the last adult in the compound to fall, at a quarter to five, because she kept him on his feet.

Her mother lived in the annex two years and went back down the hill. Nia stopped asking why at about ten, because of what her father's face did. She has the number from a birthday card. On night one she dials it for the first time, and it rings out in Morne Bay, and she doesn't let herself think about whether that's the dust or just her mother. **Status unknown**, and a thread for later: her mother is in town, which means she is in Judge's town.

## The groove

Always "the guard's girl." Allowed in, never belonging. Watching from the step.

## Power

The rarest element. Plants lean toward her, scraps rot into compost overnight, she can feel living things nearby, and she can hear the network, like whispers under the ground. She heard it first on night one, standing in the road with the corbeaux on the gate, and didn't know what it was. She is the Compound's bridge to the Hills and to Ezra. See [03](../03-power-system.md) and [05](../05-network-and-gardener.md).

**Cost:** the whispers don't stop. They are the island's dead, and some of them know her name.

## Role

She roots her father at the gate, the first in the Compound to do it, and it works. That one act splits the group: Imani's medicine versus Nia's island. The kid who never belonged is the one the island chose.

## As a lens

She opens the book. The outsider watching this loud, close group laugh at things nobody else understands, so the reader learns their jokes alongside her. By the end, the reader is in the group, and so is she, and the cost of that is the whole story.

## Relationships

- Winston: everything.
- [Kai](kai.md): the gatehouse.
- [Imani](imani.md): the only one who said her name first. The argument.
- Ezra ([supporting](supporting.md#the-hills)): the only other person who hears it.
- [The Gardener](gardener.md): wants her most. She can already hear.

## In a fight, and on a Tuesday

Rot, if she touches and means it, and she hates it. She holds the key to Winston's shotgun in the booth locker, the Compound's only gun, and fires it once in book one. Daily: compost overnight, the garden in weeks, the dead rat in the tank found by feel, which sleeper is failing before Imani knows. See [07](../07-powers-in-practice.md).
