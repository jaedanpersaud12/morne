# Lonny Baptiste, 17

**Element: Earth. Depth tier 3.**

## Core fact

His family was broke before his father got steady work and they moved up the Trace. Nobody on the Trace is rich, but the Baptistes remember being poorer. He remembers going to bed hungry as a small kid. His friends never knew.

## Who he is

Six foot three and still growing. Fit, good-looking, everybody's boy. Loves food, loves games, not the deepest thinker, always in a good mood. Carried his sleeping mother to the bed before he thought about whether he should. Locked his eleven-year-old brother Jaylen in the house and came to Imani's anyway, and said it like a confession.

## Power

Raw strength and density. He can lift a car off someone. **Cost:** it burns enormous calories. In a world where food is power, the strongest guy is also the hungriest, and feeding him becomes a political question. See [13](../13-resources-and-roles.md#food). He eats for three, and the day someone says so out loud in the kitchen is a chapter.

## Fault line

His girlfriend lives in town, on the road below the bend. When the Trace blocks its road, she is on the other side. Whether she gets inside, and what Lonny does if she doesn't, is a year-one arc. Her name is [open](../16-open-questions.md).

## Role

The carrier. Water up the hill from the Hills streams. Bodies moved. The wall built. With Danny, every run past the barricade.

## Relationships

- Jaylen, 11: his brother, awake, furious at being locked in. Attaches to Danny.
- [Danny](danny.md): partners.
- [Tori](tori.md): two doors down; little sister energy.
- [Imani](imani.md): he's the one who asks "and then?" He doesn't argue. She wishes he would.

## In a fight, and on a Tuesday

Throws a person. Throws a car door. Hits don't move him; bullets would. Daily: two drums of water from the Hills at a time, cars into the wall, the generator lifted, graves dug. Month four he carries Asha home from the ambush instead of the water. See [07](../07-powers-in-practice.md).
