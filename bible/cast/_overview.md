# Cast overview

Six friends raised on the same street in [Quarry Trace](../06-island.md#quarry-trace-house-by-house), plus the taxi driver's daughter who moved in eight months ago and was never quite one of them. Two have no power at the start.

## Depth tiers (locked)

| Tier | Who | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1: carries the book | [Imani](imani.md) (main character) | Full interior life, the most history, the biggest arc |
| 2: second POV | [Kai](kai.md), [Nia](nia.md) | Full interior life; their own chapters |
| 3: core friends | [Danny](danny.md), [Asha](asha.md), [Tori](tori.md), [Lonny](lonny.md) | One core fact each. Seen from outside until a chapter happens *to* them. |
| 4: the small one | [Micah](micah.md) | A child, not a device |
| Antagonists | [Judge](judge.md), [the Gardener](gardener.md) | Full files; short interlude chapters later |
| Supporting | [everyone else](supporting.md) | A line or two each until they earn more |

## Who gets chapters

Imani, Kai and Nia carry the book. The other four get a chapter only when something happens to them. Outsiders get short interludes starting in the second movement.

## The fault-line map

Every fault line inside the group maps onto an island-wide question. When the friends fight, the whole world's conflict plays out in miniature. Full list in [10 Conflicts](../10-conflicts.md).

```
Imani ──(medicine vs the island)── Nia
the six ──(who leads)── Troy and the other twenty-four; outsiders keep handing it to Imani
Lonny ──(close the gates)── the wall, his girlfriend outside
Kai + Asha ──(letting go)── everyone fighting to save sleepers
Tori ──(being seen)── the group she loves
Danny ──(anger)── himself
Imani ──(the clock)── her own birthday
Kai ──(the radio)── Judge, and the truth from offshore
```

## The group's private language

The six are so close their humour makes no sense to anyone else. "The goat." If outsiders saw how they talk, they'd be confused and wouldn't fit. Rules in [14 Writing rules](../14-writing-rules.md#the-groups-private-language).
