# Kai Mohan, 16

**Second POV. Element: Air (Stillness), linked with Asha. Depth tier 2.**

## Who he is

The tech one. Quiet, laid-back, in the background, respected without trying. Locs tied back, earphones in, looks at the ground beside your foot when he's looking at you. Into Western indie and rock while the island plays soca (a texture, not a plot point). Keeps the generator alive, rigs the radio, listens to the navy. Doesn't do goodbyes on the phone.

He is not "the tech guy." The tech is what he does with his hands while the rest of him is somewhere else.

## History

Their mother's long illness (cancer, a year of it, a hospital bed where the dining table used to be, a night nurse on Sundays). Their mum put both twins in a dance company as little kids. Asha stayed and competed. Kai quit at twelve, the day she came home from the first surgery, and buried it under screens and headphones. Nobody in the group has seen him dance in years.

During the worst of it he felt cornered, with no way out, and thought about ending his life. What pulled him back was love: [Nia](nia.md), and late-night talks at the gatehouse where nobody in the Compound was watching. It is why she is half inside the group.

He learned the sound of every machine in his mother's room without meaning to, the way you learn the stairs in your own house in the dark.

## His stance on their mother

He and Asha agree completely. She suffered for years, and the Sleep is the first time she has been at rest; her hands are open on the blanket for the first time in months. They are at peace with letting her go, and it still hurts. In a story full of fractures, this bond is the one that does not break. The conflict comes from outside: others judging the choice.

## Power

Stillness. He absorbs force: softens a fall, stops a punch dead, drops lightly from height. Alone he is nearly impossible to hurt, but can only release stored force slowly, as a heavy shove. If he fills up with nowhere to put it, it hurts. He stores Asha's excess momentum as anchor power: the more she moves, the more he can hold. See [Asha](asha.md#the-twins-power-flow) for the loop.

When the power comes, his body remembers how to dance.

## What he carries

- He said Imani's name to [Judge](judge.md) over an open channel on night one, and he will spend the book finding out what that cost.
- He ran the only generator on a dark island and lit his house up like a beacon.
- He controls the morphine.
- He is the one who learns, from the radio, that the quarantine is right.

## Relationships

- [Asha](asha.md): his twin and his other half, literally. No privacy when they're linked.
- [Nia](nia.md): the gatehouse. Neither of them has told anyone.
- [Imani](imani.md): the edge they both know.
- [Judge](judge.md): the voice on the handheld. A relationship conducted entirely by radio for most of a year.

## Voice on the page

Machines, sounds, counts. Short declaratives when he's working. He talks to his mother out loud when nobody's listening. He never explains himself to anyone else.

## In a fight, and on a Tuesday

Alone: absorbs anything that isn't a bullet, one stored shove, then he fills up and has to stop. With Asha: the loop, the best fighters on the island. Daily: catches what falls, steadies the generator, holds a wall section while Danny welds. The road houses fight in month five is the first time anyone sees them together, and the secret ends there. He is also the Compound's only mechanic, learning from the Harbour's apprentices and paying in ice. See [07](../07-powers-in-practice.md).
