# Olli Vale, 7

**No power. Depth tier 4.**

## Who he is

The little brother. Gap where his front teeth should be. Counts himself when the group is counted ([ch 1](../../chapters/ch01-dust/LATEST.md) L41). Puts his foot in drains. Believes anything Juno says in the bored voice.

Not a plot device: a child who has to be fed, taught and protected for three years, and who grows up inside the apocalypse. He is seven at the Sleep and ten by the end. The group's reason on the hard days, and the one who says the true thing nobody else will.

Youngest of the three Vales, raised by [Granny Dot](supporting.md#quarry-trace) with [Juno](juno.md) and [Tock](tock.md) after their mother died and their father went to Canada. He still does spelling in a school exercise book; Juno tears his pages out on night one to start the list ([ch 2](../../chapters/ch02-count/LATEST.md) L91). In August he fell off the Hales' tank stand, and Kit saw real fear on Juno's face that day ([ch 1](../../chapters/ch01-dust/LATEST.md) L169). He is a kid without a groove: too young and too untouched for a power, for now ([02](../02-the-sleep.md#outcomes-by-age)).

## Inside

**Want:** in. To be one of the six, not "the little one." On the page that is two concrete things he chases: a real job ("Olli wanted a job," [ch 5](../../chapters/ch05-spoon/LATEST.md) L75), and to be told what the goat is, which he has asked "for the hundredth time in his life" ([ch 4](../../chapters/ch04-ice/LATEST.md) L83).

**Need:** the truth about Granny, from someone he trusts, and room to grieve as a seven-year-old instead of as a helper.

**Lie:** Granny is sleeping hard, and she will wake up. He believes it because Juno said it in the bored voice, and Juno is always right ([ch 2](../../chapters/ch02-count/LATEST.md) L5).

**Fear:** that Granny will die. He says it once, out loud, at her mattress: "Is Granny going to die?" ([ch 5](../../chapters/ch05-spoon/LATEST.md) L81). Juno answers with a joke and he takes the joke.

**Wound:** he was in the kitchen when Granny went down, or got there before Juno did. On night one he is wedged between the fridge and the wall with his knees up, beside her body ([ch 1](../../chapters/ch01-dust/LATEST.md) L143). Nobody has asked him what he saw.

**How they love:** by copying and by helping. He copies Tock's walk. He does the job he is given "like it was surgery." He brings people things: the electricity bill with Mr. Hale's first name on it, run down the garden held up over his head ([ch 5](../../chapters/ch05-spoon/LATEST.md) L161).

**How it shows:** when he is scared he goes small and under something: between the fridge and the wall on night one, under the kitchen table in the early hours of day one ([ch 4](../../chapters/ch04-ice/LATEST.md) L5). When he concentrates his tongue sits between his teeth. When something is wrong he stops and looks, for a while, without asking yet (at Granny's mattress, ch 5).

**Humour:** he is the audience more than the joker. He laughs "a real one, surprised out of him" at Juno's "too rude to die" ([ch 5](../../chapters/ch05-spoon/LATEST.md) L85). His comedy is literal: counting himself in the count; asking what the goat is in the middle of everyone laughing at it.

**What breaks them:** the night he stops asking. Approved 2026-11-02 as a beat for later; **PROPOSED** until written. Every night he asks when Granny will wake, and then one night he doesn't, and that silence is how the group learns he has understood.

**Happy:** wonder. On somebody's shoulders on the road, cheering a ship he doesn't understand with everybody else ([ch 6](../../chapters/ch06-sixteen/LATEST.md) L105). Sounding out *Mr. Barnaby Hale* off a bill, the first person on the road to know the old man's name.

**Secret:** none on the page. A seven-year-old's secrets are small and literal; see Open questions.

**Book one leaves them:** [17](../17-book-one-outline.md) gives him no beat of his own. By the end of book one he is about eight. He has stopped asking when Granny will wake (**PROPOSED**), and in ch 29 Juno and Tock carry Granny to the garden; whether Olli is there, or asleep in the house, is open.

## Emotional palette

**Shows most:** wonder.
**Hides:** confusion.
**The surprise:** delayed grief. He asks every night when Granny will wake, then one night stops asking. Approved 2026-11-02; **PROPOSED** until written.

## On the page so far

- [Ch 1](../../chapters/ch01-dust/LATEST.md): comes down the hill with the six as "the little one," counting himself. That afternoon he is wedged between the fridge and the wall while Juno takes Granny's pulse on the floor, and Juno takes him by the arm (L143, L165).
- [Ch 2](../../chapters/ch02-count/LATEST.md): on the couch with the TV on, told Granny is sleeping hard, in the most bored voice Juno can find. He believes her. His spelling pages are torn out to make room for the list.
- [Ch 4](../../chapters/ch04-ice/LATEST.md): asleep under the kitchen table at ten to five. When the goat finally makes everyone laugh again, he asks "what's the goat" and nobody tells him. Sol's power first showed in his glass the night before ("Olli's glass," L247); nobody tells him that either.
- [Ch 5](../../chapters/ch05-spoon/LATEST.md): asks for a job and gets eyes: wipe, then tape, on every sleeper. Stops at Granny and asks if she's going to die; laughs at the answer. By day 4 Mr. Hale's eyes have sunk so far Olli's tape doesn't touch the lids. Finds the electricity bill and gives the dead man his first name.
- [Ch 6](../../chapters/ch06-sixteen/LATEST.md): on somebody's shoulders in the road, cheering the navy ship.
- [Ch 7](../../chapters/ch07-heat/LATEST.md): not in a scene, but his work is everywhere: Bix runs the ward jugs on Olli's old wagon, and Olli's tape is over Granny's eyes when Tock kneels by her.

## Voice

The chapters give him two lines and one word read aloud:

- "Is Granny going to die?" ([ch 5](../../chapters/ch05-spoon/LATEST.md) L81)
- "what's the goat" ([ch 4](../../chapters/ch04-ice/LATEST.md) L83)
- *Mr. Barnaby Hale.* (sounded out off a bill, [ch 5](../../chapters/ch05-spoon/LATEST.md) L161)

He talks in questions, short and direct, with no lead-in and no softening; he asks the thing the older kids are walking around. He reads and spells, and says what is written. His dialect level is not set yet (see Open questions); whatever it is, rule [14](../14-writing-rules.md#dialect-is-class) applies: never phonetic, and a shift from his normal is a sign something is wrong with him.

## Relationships

- **[Juno](juno.md):** half a mother to him. He believes anything she says in the bored voice; she has never used it on anyone older than him until Mr. Hale ([ch 5](../../chapters/ch05-spoon/LATEST.md) L59). The feeling is total trust. She gives him real work, and the lie he lives on (Granny sleeping hard) is hers. Book one has to test that trust: the night he stops asking is also the night he stops needing her to answer (**PROPOSED**).
- **[Tock](tock.md):** Tock is the fun one. Olli copies his walk. On the page so far they share the house and nothing else; Tock's attention has gone to Vee, and Olli's old wagon has gone to Bix. Whether Olli notices he has been moved down a place is open.
- **[Granny Dot](supporting.md#quarry-trace):** his bedtime. She is alive in his head as the grandmother who is too rude to die ([19](../19-imagery-and-threads.md#granny-dot-the-present-absence)). He tapes her eyes like the others' and then stops and looks at her. The feeling is plain love, without the earning Juno and Tock carry. Book one moves it from waiting to (**PROPOSED**) knowing.
- **[Sol](sol.md):** her ice first showed in his glass, and he doesn't know. Later, when Sol's secret comes out, Olli is the one who says the right thing first, because he doesn't know it's a thing ([09](../09-relationships.md#sol-and-lux-across-the-wall)).
- **Bix Hale, 11:** the nearest kid to his age among the ones with jobs. Bix runs the jugs on Olli's wagon and has Tock's side all day. Nothing between them on the page yet.
- **[Kit](kit.md):** a face at the bend. Nothing between them on the page; Kit remembers the day he fell off the tank stand by what it did to Juno's face ([ch 1](../../chapters/ch01-dust/LATEST.md) L169).

## Open questions

- Whether the children who were small when the Sleep came are different, later. Olli is where we'd see it first. (Bible [02](../02-the-sleep.md#outcomes-by-age) and [16](../16-open-questions.md) list this for babies and toddlers; at seven Olli is older than that band.) Option A: he grows up without a power and becomes the group's measure of normal. Option B: Granny's death becomes the groove, and something comes in book two or three.
- What did he see on night one? Option A: Granny lay down on purpose in front of him and he hid. Option B: he found her already down and hid before anyone came.
- How much dialect does he carry? Option A: Tock's level, because he copies Tock. Option B: Granny's finished sentences, because her house rules are the only ones he has known.
- His secret. Option A: he knows what the goat is and has never said, so he gets to keep asking. Option B: he takes the tape off Granny's eyes at night so she can see when she wakes.
- Did he know their mother? Option A: no, she died when he was a baby, so Granny is the only mother he remembers. Option B: barely, and Juno is the one who keeps her for him.
- Does he see the garden in ch 29? Option A: yes, and it is the only time he sees Granny carried. Option B: no, he sleeps through it, and the empty mattress is how he finds out.
