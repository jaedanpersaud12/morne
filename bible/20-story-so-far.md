# 20. The story so far: what's on the page

A record of what chapters 1 to 8 actually contain, kept separate from the plan in [17](17-book-one-outline.md). **Everything on this page is drawn from the chapters, with the line it comes from. Nothing here is new story material.** Planned payoffs are quoted from [17](17-book-one-outline.md) or the cast pages; where nothing is planned, the table says so. Where the chapters and the bible disagree, the chapters win and the disagreement is listed under "Conflicts found" at the end.

Day 0 is the Sunday of the Sleep. Day 1 is a Monday ([ch 4](../chapters/ch04-ice/LATEST.md) L37). Line numbers are for each chapter's LATEST.md as of 2026-11-02.

## Chapter by chapter

### 1. Dust

- **POV:** Kit. **Day:** 0, morning to about 5 p.m.
- **Summary:** Ambrose falls asleep in his taxi three times during the day and blames the dust, which has come in November instead of June ([ch 1](../chapters/ch01-dust/LATEST.md) L3-33). The six and Olli come down the hill as a noise, laugh at "the goat", and leave for the beach; Fen stops at Kit's step and promises to explain the goat later (L39-81). The pickup comes back at two because the lifeguard and the doubles lady are asleep and can't be woken; Juno tells Kit to keep her father awake (L93-103). At a quarter to five Ambrose goes under for good: slow breathing, cool skin, a skin tent that sinks slowly, 999 an open line with nobody on it, her mother's number ringing out in Morne Bay (L109-127). Kit gets him on his side in the shade and runs to the Vales', where Granny is flat on the kitchen floor and Juno is counting her pulse; Juno sends Kit back and says nobody comes past the bend (L137-165).
- **Introduced:** Kit, Ambrose, Juno, Tock, Lonny, Fen, Olli (Sol and Lyra are in the group but have no lines); Granny Vale on the floor; Mrs. Moss asleep with her Bible.
- **Reader learns:** the Trace is nine houses on a dead end up to the old quarry (L17); Kit and Ambrose moved into the flat at the bend in March, eight months ago, after her mother stayed in town (L21, L51); Juno was top of the island and is "going to be a doctor" (L57); sleepers breathe about twelve a minute (L153).
- **Last beat:** a dozen corbeaux on the flat, the taxi roof and the fence, all facing Ambrose. "The birds thought he was dead. And the birds were never wrong about that." (L175-181)
- **Against the plan:** matches [17](17-book-one-outline.md#movement-1-the-night-ch-14-day-zero). Not on the page: the shotgun, the steel box, Kit's key on a cord ([06](06-island.md#quarry-trace-house-by-house)).

### 2. Count

- **POV:** Juno. **Day:** 0, about 5 p.m. to night.
- **Summary:** Juno gives herself thirty seconds of shaking in the bathroom, then assesses Granny against the coma table from a nursing textbook she read two years ago (12 breaths, pulse 52, no response to pain) ([ch 2](../chapters/ch02-count/LATEST.md) L3-17). 999, the hospital and her father in Toronto all fail; she hangs up on his voicemail before the beep (L23-27). The others report in: every adult down, every kid fine; Lyra and Fen won't leave their mother (L31-65). Juno writes the list, remembers the Pell crib, and Tock brings the baby back alive; she shrinks the plan to the twelve houses below the bend at first light and writes *town?* (L91-137). In Dr. Moss's study she finds six IV bags for sixteen sleepers, decides to tell the truth, and puts the carton on the table in front of everyone (L141-175).
- **Introduced:** Sol, Lyra (by phone), Bix (mentioned), the Pell baby, Dr. Moss asleep in his hall, the Nettle girl.
- **Reader learns:** Granny raised the three after their mother died and their father went to Canada; "Now you have to keep it" (L19); Tock's count: 9 houses, 16 adults, 13 kids, 1 baby (L163); Juno had a bad year two years ago (L7, L13).
- **Last beat:** Kit calls: four kids at the bend, a girl with a baby, asking for "the Vale girl" as a doctor. Juno takes Dr. Moss's bag down the hill. "She was not a doctor. She went down the hill anyway." (L177-195)
- **Against the plan:** matches.

### 3. Signal

- **POV:** Fen. **Day:** 0, evening to 12:14 a.m.
- **Summary:** Fen and Lyra sit with their mother in the hospital bed in the dining room; she looks the same as before the Sleep, her hands open for the first time in months, and the twins say out loud that they don't want her to wake into her pain again ([ch 3](../chapters/ch03-signal/LATEST.md) L9-59). On the roof at ten, with his receive-only dongle, Fen finds a boy calling from the *Lady Marisol* at the fish dock; he charges his father's boat radio and answers (L63-89). The boy has two hundred kids, a broken arm and a girl who swallowed something, and asks for a doctor; Fen gives him Juno's name, and the boy names himself Cole, "Judge" (L93-115). Fen tells his mother he messed up and admits he never went back to dancing after her first surgery (L147-161). At 12:14 the grid dies and Fen starts his father's generator on the third pull (L165-181).
- **Introduced:** Lyra on the page, Mrs. Quill, Mr. Quill, Miss Tilda, Judge (Cole) by radio.
- **Reader learns:** the oxygen concentrator, mattress pump and monitor (L5); the morphine (L45, L147); Judge already sees the Trace's lights (L93); Fen used to dance (L157).
- **Last beat:** the whole island dark except the Quill house, every window lit, "one bright square in all that black". "He had done it again." (L185-191)
- **Against the plan:** matches.

### 4. Ice

- **POV:** Sol. **Day:** 1 (Monday), 4:50 a.m. to about 8 a.m.
- **Summary:** In the Vales' kitchen at ten to five Juno is counting nine formula tins; Tock names the Pell baby Vee, and Juno's "Tock, the goat" sets the room off ([ch 4](../chapters/ch04-ice/LATEST.md) L5-85). Sol and Lonny walk the twelve houses below the bend: the dogs don't bark, Miss Orla's house has a note from nine-year-old Tam who has taken his grandmother toward the hospital in a wheelbarrow, and tap water goes fridge-cold in Sol's hands (L89-161). At the sixth house Wren, with her brother Pip, asks to come up the Trace and Lonny says yes without asking anyone (L163-189). Back at the Vales', Vee has a high fever and nothing is cold; Sol freezes a bucket of tank water in front of Juno, Tock and Lonny, and the cost is a burning ache, shaking and a raging thirst (L193-255). Juno doesn't ask what else Sol can do (L249-251).
- **Introduced:** Wren, Pip, Tam (by his note), Vee by name; Kit seen on the gallery with Ambrose on a mattress (L99).
- **Reader learns:** Vee was really Velora, found "weeks" later (L61); Sol's mother runs the ice plant (L151); Sol has "a door like that too, in town" she has never knocked on (L165); the cold room is where Sol goes when the world is too loud (L221).
- **Last beat:** Kit shouts from the bend; a boy of nine at the bottom of the hill with his grandmother in a wheelbarrow (L257-263).
- **Against the plan:** 17 gives the chapter as "Sol / Lonny"; on the page it is Sol's POV only. 17 has "Lonny's girl, alive, outside"; on the page Wren and Pip come inside.

### 5. Spoon

- **POV:** Juno. **Days:** night of day 1 to day 5.
- **Summary:** Lonny carries fifteen sleepers into the Mosses' front room; Mrs. Quill stays home at Lyra's insistence and Kit keeps Ambrose on the gallery without saying why ([ch 5](../chapters/ch05-spoon/LATEST.md) L5-7). Juno tests swallowing with a teaspoon: seven cough, and she gives six bags to Mrs. Pell, Mrs. Nettle, Mr. Finch, the wheelbarrow grandmother, Mr. Pell and Granny, and puts herself on Mr. Hale with the spoon (L9-59). By day 4 the ward smells, the turning grid runs every two hours, Olli tapes eyelids, and every bag has run dry; Mr. Hale gets the most water and gets worse (L65-105). He dies at 5:12 a.m. on day 5 after a night of irregular breathing (L107-123). Lonny digs the grave under the lime tree behind number nine, four feet down to rock, through soil threaded with something white and fine (L141-163).
- **Introduced:** Ida Moss, praying over the ward; Mr. Barnaby Hale, named off an electricity bill (L161).
- **Reader learns:** how hand care works and how far short it falls (L69-71: eight cups a day needed, not four given); Juno's "She's too rude to die. God doesn't want her." (L83); the red cricket ball (L175).
- **Last beat:** Juno writes the next names in the order the numbers give; the first is Granny's. She looks down at the hospital, eleven kilometres away, "in a town where people already knew her name" (L203-211).
- **Against the plan:** matches. 17's cost audit flags the Granny-over-Mr.-Hale choice as "cover, not cost".

### 6. Sixteen

- **POV:** Fen. **Days:** night of day 5 to night of day 7.
- **Summary:** Judge calls every night at nine; on the night of the funeral he already knows about the grave from "a boy who could hear a pin drop on that hill," and asks for Juno for the broken arm ([ch 6](../chapters/ch06-sixteen/LATEST.md) L3-53). Fen goes to the Vales', sees Juno sitting still with her hand flat on the page, and walks home without knocking (L57-61). On the seventh morning a grey ship with no AIS signal sits on the horizon, and channel 16 carries a navy quarantine loop: vessels "will be stopped" (L67-99). The road cheers; Juno asks if they said anything, Fen says "They're not talking to us," and Juno tells the road "Somebody's coming" (L101-143). That night Judge says he has told the Harbour the ship is there to keep them in, guesses Fen hasn't told Juno, and threatens to send somebody up "to ask her nice" (L161-185).
- **Introduced:** Ears (unnamed: "a boy", L33); Cookie and Nell by name (L43); the navy, by voice.
- **Reader learns:** the generator schedule and fuel (L11-15); Mrs. Quill holds 94 on room air and Fen is not thinking about what it means (L11); Fen's own worst stretch last year, behind a locked door (L59), and that "one more day" was once the whole plan (L195); the *24 KIDS. SEND HELP* sheet on the quarry floor (L151).
- **Last beat:** a drone hangs thirty feet over the only lit house, red light unblinking, then slides away toward the ship (L201-211).
- **Against the plan:** matches. 17 lists "the fish market"; Fen sees it only through binoculars (L69-71).

### 7. Heat

- **POV:** Tock. **Days:** 6 and 7.
- **Summary:** Tock has had a fever for three days and hidden it so Juno won't take Vee; on day 6 the gas runs out, the Pells' regulator won't seat, Vee screams, and he grabs the pot: it boils in his hands, the handles melt, and he leaves a burned handprint on Granny's counter ([ch 7](../chapters/ch07-heat/LATEST.md) L3-67). He scrubs it, remembers Granny's "fire belongs to the devil", and keeps away from Vee all day (L71-97). On day 7, while the road cheers the ship, he kneels by Granny in the ward to pray it out and cracks the floor tile; Ida sees (L99-131). Ida tells him the devil doesn't boil water for sleepers, and they find that anger gives a violent boil and thinking of Vee gives a steady one he can hold for a minute, at the cost of dizziness and thirst (L135-195). That night he takes Vee back (L197-199).
- **Introduced:** Ida properly; Tock's full name, Tobias (L77).
- **Reader learns:** the ward needs forty litres of boiled water a day (L9); Juno's list of thirteen cylinders, one a day, which "won't last to Christmas" (L21, L169); Tock's old temper and the hollow door at twelve (L75).
- **Last beat:** at the kitchen table Fen admits Judge asked twice and wants Juno tomorrow or he'll "ask nice"; Tock's anger burns two handprints into Granny's mahogany table; Juno looks at the gas cylinder and the pot and asks, "How hot can you get it?" (L203-235)
- **Against the plan:** matches. 17's PROPOSED revision (open on the pot already melted) has not been applied; the pot is on the page.

### 8. Market

- **POV:** Juno. **Day:** 8.
- **Summary:** After a show of hands, Juno and Lonny walk the eleven kilometres to Morne Bay; at the junction the Harker boys sit on the gas station roof and the youngest, Rust, leaves a brown mark where his hand rested ([ch 8](../chapters/ch08-market/LATEST.md) L7-39). Nell takes them to Judge on the *Lady Marisol*, where his father sleeps under a tarp (L61-93). Juno resets the fourteen-year-old's eight-day-old break on the second pull, and can do nothing for Ria, seven, who swallowed a watch battery: "You're what we got," Judge says (L95-149). Walking her out, Judge gives his numbers, his twelve guns, and tells her the Trace has Ambrose's gun and Kit has the key, because "Ears hears everything"; he asks her to stay, she refuses, and he says he'll come up "sometimes, to talk" (L153-191). He names the girl who makes ice and gives her a box of twelve IV bags from the hospital store: "Gift." (L195-213)
- **Introduced:** Judge in person (Cole Mayers), Nell, Rust and the Harker boys, Judge's father, Ria, Ears by name.
- **Reader learns:** the Harbour feeds 310 (L161); rice for five weeks, the church standpipe running for no known reason (L161); the police station's twelve guns, taken on the second day (L169); Nell's list of twenty-two names (L153).
- **Last beat:** Juno passes Kit at the bend and looks past her at "the bedroom where the steel box was"; she puts the bags in the ward without saying where they came from, and lies awake with the notebook on her chest and Ria's name smudged grey on her wrist, "a secret now, like Fen".
- **Against the plan:** matches, with more in it than 17 lists (Rust, Ria, the gun, the ice, the gift).

### 9. Ground

- **POV:** Kit. **Days:** 8 (evening) to the morning of 10.
- **Summary:** Juno walks past Kit's lifted hand and looks straight at the bedroom; Lonny brings the grid with Ambrose's name on it and an offer: one of Judge's twelve bags, if he comes up to the ward. Kit refuses. Alone, she opens the steel box: shotgun, fare book, eleven shells (one gone, she doesn't want to know where), and works out that somebody told Juno. Morning: Bix brings Vee down ("Tock says ask Kit"); in the ward Ida says it was Sunday, God put them down to listen, and the Nettle girl believes her. Kit walks to the junction with two hundred dollars from the fare tin for formula; the Harkers take the money "because money was still money" and Rust thanks her for a free drop from her father. On the kerb a barefoot boy, Moth, knows her name: his grandfather is "in the ground, four days now, not dead", breathing twelve, not getting thin. "You said that last time" doesn't work on him. That night her mother's number does nothing, three times; she says a year of things to a dead phone, then digs the one soft strip of yard (where the wash water ran) and drags Ambrose down three steps and across the dirt on a sheet, alone. Warm soil, white threads. By dawn threads lie against his left wrist; his hand closes once, as on the wheel at a red light. The corbeaux leave the taxi roof and don't come back. The low sound she has heard since night one is many voices, not a tap, and one of them says something like her name. It soothes her; that is what frightens her. Last beat: a door up the hill, Juno coming down fast.
- **Introduced:** Moth (in person), the Harker shop door with no handle (Rust's streak), the steel box's contents.
- **Reader learns:** eleven shells; the sound under the Trace is voices and has been there since the first night; Ambrose's last fare entry (Moss, Clinic, 40, then the pen drags off).
- **Against the plan:** as the sheet, plus Lonny's bag offer (gives the refusal a cost) and the formula errand.

### 10. Skin

- **POV:** Juno. **Days:** 10 (dawn) to the dawn of 11.
- **Summary:** Juno comes down to bring Ambrose up and finds him in the turned earth. She does the full table on him, which she hasn't done on Granny in two days: twelve, fifty-two, and the skin tent that springs back. Threads at the wrist. "That's a fungal infection." Kit: "Pinch his hand again." / "You can't take back the bags either." Juno walks up without answering and understands she did every line on the one sleeper who didn't need her. Granny is at ten, irregular, eleven-second pauses, the Mr. Hale pattern; Juno hangs the first of the twelve bags on her and writes it as a number. Olli, who came down for Mr. Wick's eyes, is the leak; by noon eight kids stand at Kit's fence. Ida: "Then she buried a living man." The Nettle girl asks to put her mother in the ground; no. Kitchen, twenty people: Ida's Sunday theory against Juno's "it's age, the brain isn't finished until twenty-five", said like it was whole; Fen says nothing; Tock: "Then what does it do to the finished ones?" Nobody answers. Juno rules, in the on-duty voice, that nobody goes in the ground until they know what it is. Kit, in the doorway: "I didn't ask you", takes her father's thermos and leaves. Night with Granny: palm turns up at midnight (the report-card hand); Juno's mouth stops on the third line of the prayer. Dawn: Ears stands exactly on the line of the bend, head tilted, already knowing she was coming, with a note: "Vale" in Nell's hand outside, Judge's inside: *Ask Fen what the ship said.* Kit watches from the step. Neither speaks. Fen's light is still on.
- **Introduced:** Ears in person (unnamed on the page), the pauses in Granny's breathing.
- **Reader learns:** Ambrose's skin springs back where everyone in the ward tents; Juno now knows there was a ship and that Fen heard it; the road knows a sleeper is in the ground.
- **Against the plan:** sheet's five scenes merged to three; title changed from "Turgor" to "Skin".
