# 07. Powers in practice: numbers, daily use, combat, and the unaligned

The power system in [03](03-power-system.md) says what powers are. This file says what people do with them: how many kids have them, what they're for on a Tuesday, what happens when they fight, and what the island looks like once desperate kids with strange gifts stop belonging to anyone.

**Why the Compound has so many.** One in ten is the island rate. Eight of twenty-seven is nearly one in three. Palmyra Gardens sits on the blast wound where the network's roots are densest ([05](05-network-and-gardener.md#why-now)). The Hills, over the deep roots, run high too. The Harbour runs low (one in ten) but has ten times the kids, so in absolute terms it has fifteen times the Compound's powered population. **The Compound is never the strongest faction. It is the most concentrated.**

**The eight in the Compound:** [Danny](cast/danny.md), [Lonny](cast/lonny.md), [Kai](cast/kai.md), [Asha](cast/asha.md), [Tori](cast/tori.md), [Nia](cast/nia.md), Troy (awakens month three: Earth, a hardening of the skin, which makes him harder to argue with and easier for the Gardener to use), and the Lall girl, 12 (month five: a small Water gift, she can find water underground by standing still, which is the most useful power in the dry season and belongs to a child).

**Awakening rate over time.** Month one: 3 in the Compound (Tori, Danny, Lonny). Month three: 6. Month six: 8. After that, only the second bloom changes it.

**The Harbour's roster** (names in [supporting](cast/supporting.md#the-harbours-powered-kids)): Judge (the sea), Dev and three battery kids (Lightning), Marsh (fresh water from seawater, at the cost of her kidneys), Big Shawn (Earth, 18, fading), Cookie (Fire, feeds two hundred), Ears (Air, the lookout). No healers.

**What the Compound doesn't have, and the Harbour does:** Lightning. A Lightning kid can jump-start a dead engine, charge a car battery by holding the terminals, and run a radio off their own body. Judge has four of them, which is why the Harbour has power and the Compound has one generator and a shrinking fuel can ([13](13-resources-and-roles.md#fuel)). The Compound's long game is solar; the Harbour's is Dev.

**Healers.** Imani has no power. The Hills have two Life/decay kids who can take pain out of a person and into themselves, which is not healing, but looks like it, and is what the Hills' faith grows from. The Compound's medicine is books and hands. That asymmetry is a trade route and a temptation.

**Mechanics.** No power replaces knowing how an engine works. Kai learns from manuals and from the two garage apprentices at the Harbour, who teach him in exchange for ice. A Lightning kid can start a car; only a mechanic can keep it running.

## Combat

Nobody is a superhero. Danny can weld; he cannot throw fireballs. Fights are short, ugly, and mostly fought with the island's actual weapons.

### The rules

1. **A power is thirty seconds.** Then shaking, hunger, and in Danny's case a fever. A fight that lasts longer than that is cutlasses and bats.
2. **Most fighting is ordinary.** Cutlasses (every yard has one), cricket bats, bottles, car bonnets as shields, Carnival costume pieces as armour (chest plates, headdress wire, and it is not a joke when you're looking at it). One powered kid per side, maybe two.
3. **Guns change everything, and the Harbour has them.** Judge took the police station in week one. Forty guns on the whole island and he holds more than half. The Compound has one shotgun and eleven shells. Every fight with the Harbour is a fight the Compound can only win by not having it.
4. **Terrain and value beat force.** The Compound survives because it's on a hill behind an eight-foot wall with a welded gate, and because Judge needs Imani's hands and Tori's ice more than he needs the hill. The day that stops being true is the siege.
5. **Killing with a power costs more than the kid expects.** The network notices. The first time a powered kid kills someone, something in them goes quiet for days, and later that kid's power may turn up in someone else. See [03](03-power-system.md#rules-we-can-use-later) on echoes.
6. **Injuries last.** A cutlass wound in year one is weeks of Imani's work and a real chance of dying of infection. Nobody walks it off.
7. **The twins are the exception,** and the reason the Compound is feared out of proportion to its size. Together they are the best fighters on the island, because the harder you hit them the stronger they get. Alone, each is beatable, which everyone eventually works out.

## The unaligned: crews, raiders, and kids with ridiculous powers

Between the factions is everyone who won't join one. On a small island that is maybe a hundred kids by month six, in a dozen crews of three to eight, and everyone knows their names, because everyone went to school with them.

**How they form.** A house empties (the sleepers die, or get rooted, or the kids just leave). Two or three kids with nothing to lose find a car with petrol in it. By month three they've learned that a kid with a strange gift can walk into a house and take what's there, and nobody comes. By month six some of them have stopped pretending it's survival.

**What they want.** Food, batteries, fuel, medicine, and by year two, each other. They don't want territory. They want to not be told what to do, which is the one thing every faction is offering.

**The island version of Mad Max.** No desert, no highways. Instead: cars run until the petrol goes bad, then bicycles and the gas truck, which one crew keeps alive for a year. Carnival costume pieces as armour. Dogs, which the crews feed and the factions don't. Graffiti on the coast road: a crew's name on every house they've emptied. A kid in a welding mask who never takes it off. Cricket pads. A boy who rides the roof of the gas truck with a cutlass and a Lightning girl beside him keeping its battery alive.

### How crews touch the factions

- **The Compound** sits above the Junction. The road is contested from month one. The Compound's gate exists because of the Mohammed brothers.
- **The Harbour** treats crews as pests until the Mas Camp hits a fish convoy, and then Judge hunts them, and the crews learn to fear the dock boys more than each other. Some join him. Rust never does.
- **The Hills** get raided for food and respond by rooting the raiders who die there, which is the first time anyone sees a rooted kid, and the Gardener comes from somewhere near that.
- **The Gardener** recruits from the crews. Kids with nothing and nobody are the easiest to offer a family in the ground.
- **The crews are the island's weather.** Smoke on the coast road is a crew. A price going up at the Harbour is a crew. A road house burned is a crew. The protagonists rarely see the cause; they see the ripple ([08](08-factions-and-politics.md#how-small-things-move-the-world)).

| Month 2 to 3 | Crews form. The Junction tries the gate. Danny's handprint. |
| Month 4 to 6 | The Mas Camp. Raids on the road and the Hills. The Compound builds its wall. Judge starts hunting crews. |
| Month 6 to 12 | The Harbour siege. Guns used in anger for the first time. The island learns the Compound's twins can't be beaten and that the Compound can be starved. |

| Year three | The second bloom. A hundred new powered kids and nobody to teach them. Open. |

## The rule for chapters

Powers on the page do work before they do damage. A chapter where Danny only burns someone is a chapter where we forgot he welded the gate that morning.
