# 17. Book one outline

**One line:** a girl who has spent her life earning the right to be loved tries to keep her grandmother alive through a year where the island keeps offering her an easier way, while her friends keep refusing to let her do it alone.

**Spine:** the sleepers. Beds or the ground. Juno's medicine is the old world; Kit's garden is the new one. The book is Juno losing that argument slowly, one bed death at a time, while being right about everything else.

**Length:** about 30 chapters, 3,000 words each, around 90,000 words. POV rotates among [Juno](cast/juno.md), [Fen](cast/fen.md) and [Kit](cast/kit.md); the other friends get one chapter each when something happens to them; short interludes from Judge and the Hills from movement 3 on.

### Side arcs under the main plot

- **Lonny on the road** (ch 17 to 26): off-page for nine chapters. Keeping twelve houses alive with his hands. The Junction learns not to cross him. The girl's mother dies in her bed anyway. He comes back for Bix's funeral and never says what the road was like.
- **Ambrose in the ground**: rooted in the yard at the bend, slowly changing. Every Kit chapter checks on him in one line. By movement 5 his eyes are open and track nothing, and the Gardener says he's dreaming of her.
- **The Quill parents**: their mother in the garden by ch 18. Their father still in a bed in the hall, whom the twins never decided anything about. The sleeper nobody talks about.
- **Kit's mother**: a Morne Bay number that rang out. In movement 3 Nell mentions a woman asleep in a flat on the coast road with Kit's surname on the mailbox. Kit doesn't go. Book two.
- **Cass**: from "why do six friends decide" to a hardened kid in the gate to the Gardener's man inside the walls. His mother's bed death is the hinge. The reader sees every step and agrees with half of them.
- **Ida**: never roots her father. By ch 29 hers is the last bed in the Trace, and her father is alive. The counter-argument the book never resolves.
- **Juno's father in Toronto**: awake, and the outside world knows where his children are. A voicemail in ch 2, a name the scientist says on the channel in ch 22.
- **Marsh**: fresh water from the sea, kidneys as the price. The Harbour's answer to the dry season, and the first person on the island to be used up by her gift on purpose, by someone who knew.
- **Big Hob**: eighteen, fading. What the age line looks like from the outside, a year before Juno reaches it.
