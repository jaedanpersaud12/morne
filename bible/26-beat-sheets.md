# 26. Beat sheets

Scene-by-scene plans for chapters not yet written. A plan, not prose: the lines under "must land" are suggestions for the shape of a moment, to be rewritten in your voice or thrown out. When a chapter is written, delete its sheet and update [20 The story so far](20-story-so-far.md).

## Chapter 9 — Kit — working title "Ground"

**POV:** [Kit](cast/kit.md), close third. Her first chapter since ch 1.
**Days:** 8 (evening) to the morning of 10.
**Length:** about 2,800 words, three scenes ([14](14-writing-rules.md#chapters)).
**What the chapter does:** brings the opening POV back as a person, plants the shotgun properly, voices the first wrong theory, and ends with the act that splits the group: Kit roots her father alone, without asking.
**Sources:** [17](17-book-one-outline.md#movement-2-the-scramble-ch-511-weeks-16) row 9, the proposed "Box" scene and "the theory, voiced and wrong" ([17](17-book-one-outline.md#proposed-insertions-2026-11-02)), [04](04-sleepers.md) (what rooting looks like), [05](05-network-and-gardener.md), [Moth](cast/supporting.md#the-hills).

### Where Kit is when it opens

Eight days on the gallery at the bend. Ambrose on a mattress in the shade, on his side, cushion under his head. She kept him out of the ward and never said why. The corbeaux have sat on the taxi roof since day zero. She has the key to the steel box on a cord round her neck. She doesn't know that Judge knows, or that Juno now knows ([ch 8](../chapters/ch08-market/LATEST.md) L147, L193). Her mother's number rang out on night one. Fen still owes her the goat.

### Scene 1 — the box (day 8, dusk)

This replaces the outline's "Box" insertion and keeps the book in order. It runs the same evening as the end of ch 8, from the other side of Juno's glance.

| Beat | What happens | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open | Kit on the gallery, counting Ambrose's breaths. Twelve. The group comes back up the hill from the Harbour with the bag. She lifts a hand. Juno looks past her, through the open door, at the bedroom, and keeps walking. | Mirrors ch 8 L193 from the inside. Kit clocks the look and doesn't know what it means yet. |
| Obstacle | Lonny comes back down with a mattress and Juno's turning schedule. He's kind: "You're one of us, you know." She doesn't believe him and doesn't argue, because he carried her father's weight once already. | Her want (keep Ambrose at the bend, by her own hands) meets the road's kindness, which is worse than an order. |
| The box | After Lonny goes, she opens the steel box under the bed for the first time. The shotgun. The shells: eleven, she counts, and nobody taught her to. The fare book on top in his neat capitals. She closes it. Then she realises what Juno was looking at. Somebody told her. | **Plants the gun at last** ([25](25-revision-notes.md)). It first appears in the hands of the one person who doesn't want it. Her first kept thing is knowing that they know. |
| Turn | Back on the gallery in the dark, the ground under the boards isn't silent: a sound under the dogs, under the generator up the hill, like a tap left running in another house. She decides it's grief. | The whispers, deniable, the way Sol's glass was. |

**Must land:** the count of eleven, done without thinking (her tell is counting, [Kit](cast/kit.md)). Juno's glance, which she can't stop thinking about.

### Scene 2 — the ward and the junction (day 9)

| Beat | What happens | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The ward | Kit walks up to the Mosses' for the first time in days, for water or formula. Ida, over a sleeper, says it was Sunday: everybody who fell was in church or had the service on. "God put them down to listen." The Nettle girl believes it at once. Kit's father had the service on the car radio. She doesn't believe it and can't say why. | The first theory, voiced and wrong ([17](17-book-one-outline.md#9-and-10--the-theory-voiced-and-wrong)). Kit is an outsider in the ward too. Show the rows she has kept Ambrose out of, and let the reader see her reason without her saying it. |
| Errand | Formula is low. Kit offers to go to the junction, because nobody else wants to and because it gets her off the road. | Domains sorting themselves; Kit takes the edge job. |
| Moth | At the junction, past the Harkers' padlocked pump, a Hills boy, fourteen, barefoot, sits on the kerb as if he's waiting for her. He asks for "the taxi man's daughter" and then uses her name. He says his grandfather (Old Man Tobit) is in the ground and is not dead. He says the ground will take her father too, if she asks it. He says it plainly, the way you'd give directions. | Moth is the only other person who hears it ([supporting](cast/supporting.md#the-hills)). Near him the running-tap sound is louder. |
| Sequel | She walks back with two tins and doesn't tell anyone about Moth. | The secret grows. |

**Must land:** Moth not selling it. The scariest thing about him is that he's calm. Kit's dry deflation ("You said that last time.") meeting someone it doesn't work on.

### Scene 3 — the root (night 9 into morning 10)

| Beat | What happens | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The number | Kit dials her mother's number again on the dead phone. It doesn't connect. The rage she has never let out ([Kit](cast/kit.md): what she hides) comes out at the phone, not at a person. | The hidden emotion, once, privately, and it turns into resolve. |
| The ground | She turns a patch of the yard where the taxi's back wheels have always stood, with Ambrose's own spade. The soil is warmer than the air. | [04](04-sleepers.md): turned soil, not lawn. The yard is the only ground she has ever been inside first. |
| The weight | She moves him alone. Fifteen years old, a grown man, the sheet, the gallery steps, one step at a time. It costs her. No Lonny. | "With her own hands" made literal. The decision has to cost before it works ([14](14-writing-rules.md#decisions-cost)). |
| Laying down | On his side, cushion under his head the way Juno taught everyone, on the bare earth. She sits beside him with her knees up and the key against her chest. | Her love is keeping watch. |
| Dawn | By first light, fine white threads at his wrist where it touches the ground. His breathing: twelve, steady. The corbeaux lift off the taxi roof and don't come back. | The corbeaux attend every death ([14](14-writing-rules.md)); leaving here says something has changed that isn't death. |
| Last beat | The running-tap sound is not a tap. Under it, very quiet, something that sounds like her name. She doesn't let herself think about whose voice. Up the hill, a door: someone is coming down the road. | The cost: the whispers know her name ([Kit](cast/kit.md)). Hands off to ch 10 (Juno finds them). |

**Must land:** the comfort. The whispers soothe her, and that is what frightens her (approved surprise, [Kit](cast/kit.md)). Don't explain the rooting; show threads and breath.

### Setups this chapter pays or plants

- **Pays:** the steel box and Juno's glance (ch 8), the key on the cord, Ambrose on the gallery "without explaining" (ch 5 to 8), the corbeaux on the taxi (ch 1).
- **Plants:** eleven shells (fired once, siege, ch 25), Moth, the whispers knowing her name (ch 28, the Gardener), Ida's faith against rooting ([04](04-sleepers.md): Ida refuses on religious grounds).
- **Not yet:** the goat; Fen and the taxi nights (a glance at the taxi at most); Juno's envy, which starts in ch 10 from Juno's side.

### Who knows what after ch 9

Update Who knows in Studio when it's written: Kit knows that Juno knows about the gun; nobody knows about Moth; nobody knows about the rooting until ch 10.

### Checks before saving

- Three scenes; "said" only; Kit's dialect lighter than Ambrose's and heavier than Juno's ([21](21-style-sheet.md)).
- Count the tics ([14](14-writing-rules.md#tics-to-count-before-saving-a-version)): counting is Kit's, so ration the other characters' counting in this chapter.
- No glow and no magic language for the rooting. White threads, cool firm skin, steady breath.

## Chapter 10 — Juno — working title "Turgor"

**POV:** [Juno](cast/juno.md), close third.
**Days:** 10 (dawn) to the morning of 11.
**Length:** about 3,000 words, three scenes.
**What the chapter does:** Juno meets the first thing on the island that works better than her book, and it belongs to Kit. The Trace's central argument starts, nobody wins it, and two girls stop speaking. Granny gets worse. Juno learns about the ship from the wrong person.
**Sources:** [17](17-book-one-outline.md) row 10 and "the theory, voiced and wrong", [09](09-relationships.md#juno-and-kit-the-argument), [04](04-sleepers.md), [19](19-imagery-and-threads.md) secrets ledger (the ship), cost audit item 6.

### Where Juno is when it opens

Day 10. Twelve bags in the Mosses' front room and a secret about where they came from. Granny first on the list of who dies next. Ria's name on her wrist. She hasn't slept in a bed since night one. She looked at the steel box in Kit's bedroom on day 8 and kept walking; she has said nothing to Kit about it.

### Scene 1 — the yard (day 10, dawn)

| Beat | What happens | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open | Juno coming down the road at first light, the "someone" from the end of ch 9. She's going to bring Ambrose up to the ward, finally, because the turning schedule has a gap in it and she's tired of the gap. | Picks up ch 9's last beat from the other side. Her want is order. |
| The empty gallery | Mattress, cushion, no Ambrose. A second of pure fear (he's dead, Kit's buried him) before she sees them in the yard. | The worst reading first, the way Juno thinks. |
| The exam | She kneels and does what she does: the coma table. Breathing twelve. Pulse. Then she pinches the skin on the back of his hand, and it **springs back**. In ch 1 it sank slowly. Nobody in her ward has skin that springs back. | The book's own test turned against her. The single physical fact the chapter turns on. Keep it clinical: no wonder, just the number. |
| The threads | White threads at the wrist, into the soil. Her book has a word for this: fungal infection. She says it out loud. Kit says, "He's fine." Both are true. | Neither is wrong ([09](09-relationships.md#juno-and-kit-the-argument)). |
| The argument, quiet | It stays low because it's dawn and they are both tired. Juno: you don't know what it is, you can't take it back. Kit: you don't know what it is either, and yours are dying. Kit's hand is flat on the soil the way Juno's lies flat on the exercise book. | Juno's tell, mirrored in Kit. **PROPOSED envy beat:** Juno sees it and doesn't name what she feels. |
| Turn | Juno says she has to tell the others. Kit: "Tell them what?" Juno walks back up. Halfway, she realises she checked Ambrose more carefully than she has checked Granny in two days. | The decision and its cost in one turn. |

**Must land:** the skin springing back. Juno's steadiest face, which only ever means something is badly wrong.

### Scene 2 — the table (day 10)

| Beat | What happens | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Granny's numbers | First thing back: Granny. Breaths ten, irregular. A pause and then a gasp, the pattern Mr. Hale had the night before he died ([ch 5](../chapters/ch05-spoon/LATEST.md)). Juno writes it down and doesn't let herself read it. | Granny failing, the outline's other half of the chapter. The arithmetic is the horror ([19](19-imagery-and-threads.md)). |
| Word gets out | Olli was on the gallery and saw Juno's face, so by noon the road knows. Half of them walk down to look at Ambrose. Ida calls it unholy. The Nettle girl asks if her mother can go in the ground today. | The Trace splits along lines it already had. Plants secret rooting ([04](04-sleepers.md): "some do it in secret"). |
| The theory | At the kitchen table, everyone in. Ida says Sunday again: God put them down to listen. Juno answers with the nursing book: adolescent brains are still wiring until the mid-twenties. "It got into something that's still being built. It couldn't get into something finished." She's half right and says it like it's whole. | The theory, voiced and wrong ([17](17-book-one-outline.md#9-and-10--the-theory-voiced-and-wrong)). Juno gets to be the clever one exactly once in the chapter. |
| Two listeners | Fen thinks about the ship and says nothing. Tock asks the only good question: "Then what does it do to the finished ones?" Nobody answers. | Fen's silence goes on the bill for scene 3. |
| The ruling | Juno says nobody puts anybody in the ground until they know what it is. Not a vote this time; she just says it, in the on-duty voice. Kit, at the door: "I didn't ask you." | She breaks her own rule (the six decide by domain, [08](08-factions-and-politics.md)) and acts like the leader she keeps saying she isn't. Cass (ch 11) notices. |

**Must land:** Tock's question, left hanging. Kit's line at the door, said flatly, without a fight in it.

### Scene 3 — the night and the note (night 10 into morning 11)

| Beat | What happens | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The spoon | Juno alone with Granny, the spoon, the rhythm of the pauses. She says the prayer under her breath, the one she knows every word of and couldn't say in ch 5. She gets halfway. | Her love is the job. The prayer is the closest she comes to asking for help. |
| The door | At three, she goes to the gallery and looks down the hill at Kit's yard, the dark square of turned soil. She stands there long enough to count to thirty, twice. She goes back in. | The thirty-seconds rule turned outward: she doesn't let the wanting have as long as it wants. Granny stays in the bed. This is the decision that costs most later. |
| The note | Dawn. A Harbour kid at the bend gate with a folded page from Judge (Ears is the obvious messenger). One line: *Ask Fen what the ship said.* | Juno learns about the ship from Judge, not Fen, as the ledger requires ([19](19-imagery-and-threads.md)). Judge never lies; he hurts with the truth. |
| Last beat | She goes down for water past Kit's yard. Kit is on the step. For the first time in eight months, Juno doesn't say "Morning, Kit." Neither of them speaks. The note is in her pocket. Up the hill, Fen's light is still on. | They stop speaking. Pays "Morning, Kit" ([Juno](cast/juno.md)). Hands off to ch 11 (Fen, between them). |

**Must land:** the missing "Morning, Kit". Don't explain it; the reader has heard her say it every time.

### Setups this chapter pays or plants

- **Pays:** "someone coming down the road" (ch 9), the skin tent that sank slowly (ch 1), the coma table and the nursing book (ch 2), Mr. Hale's breathing (ch 5), Fen's ship secret (ch 6; the ledger's "by ch 10 at the latest"), "Morning, Kit" (ch 1 onwards), Juno's glance at the steel box (she still says nothing; Kit still doesn't know she knows).
- **Plants:** the Nettle girl and secret rooting, Juno's ruling (Cass's "why do six decide for thirty", ch 11), the brain-plasticity theory the kids believe for most of book one, Tock's question, Juno's envy, Granny's pauses (they don't stop until ch 29).
- **Not yet:** Juno confronting Fen (that's ch 11, seen by Lonny); the twelve bags' source; any hint of Juno's power.

### Who knows what after ch 10

Juno knows about the ship from Judge (and knows Fen kept it). The whole road knows Ambrose is rooted. Nobody knows about Moth. Kit still doesn't know Juno knows about the gun.

### Checks before saving

- Three scenes; "said" only; Juno's dialect near-standard on duty, lighter with the friends ([21](21-style-sheet.md)).
- Her tells, rationed: "okay" in threes once at most, the glasses once, the hand flat on the page once (and once on Kit, in scene 1).
- Kit gets no interior here. Everything she feels is on her face or in her four words.
- No villain. If a reader picks a side by the end, the chapter is off.

## Chapter 11 — Lonny — working title "Yes."

**POV:** [Lonny](cast/lonny.md), close third. His first chapter. Replaces the Fen row in [17](17-book-one-outline.md) (the proposal there, now taken); every beat of the old Fen chapter survives, seen from outside.
**Days:** 11 (morning) to the morning of 12.
**Length:** about 3,000 words, three scenes.
**What the chapter does:** the second bed death, and it's someone's mother with a son awake beside her. Cass asks the question the Trace never settles. Lonny keeps his first secret. Earth arrives, deniable. The cost of Juno's ruling in ch 10 is paid by somebody else.
**Sources:** [17 insertion "11 — Lonny"](17-book-one-outline.md#11--lonny--replaces-the-current-ch-11-fen), [Lonny](cast/lonny.md), [09](09-relationships.md#lonny-and-the-girl-on-the-road), [04](04-sleepers.md) ("bed deaths are the cost of not rooting… that someone is Juno").

### Fixes this sheet makes (PROPOSED)

- **Cass's arrival** ([20](20-story-so-far.md) item 8): Cass and his mother aren't in ch 1 to 8 and she isn't on the ward list. Here they arrive on day 11, not night one. Cass has kept her alive alone on the road for ten days with a spoon and sugar water, and comes up because the road says the Trace has a doctor and new bags. Update [supporting](cast/supporting.md#quarry-trace) ("night one" becomes "day 11") once this is written.
- **Wren's mother:** alive on day 11 only if someone has been tending her. Wren has gone down every afternoon to spoon and turn her. Today she can't, because Juno put her on the ward rota.

### Where Lonny is when it opens

Both his parents in the rows at the Mosses'. He carried every one of them in. He dug Mr. Hale's grave. He said yes to Wren on day 1 and nobody has called it. He carried Judge's twelve bags up the hill and knows what they are. He hasn't once argued with Juno. He is hungry all the time and thinks that's just him.

### Scene 2 — the gallery and the road (night 11)

| Beat | What happens | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cass | Cass and Lonny on the gallery between turns. Cass has heard about Ambrose and the ruling. "Why do six people who grew up together decide for thirty?" Lonny has no answer, because he agrees. | The question goes first to the one of the six who can't defend it ([17](17-book-one-outline.md)). |
| Wren | Wren at the gate in the dark. She couldn't get down today, and her mother hasn't been turned or spooned since yesterday. Lonny says he'll go. Juno, from the doorway: "The road is the road." | His ch 4 yes comes due. Juno isn't cruel; she's counting carers. |
| Going anyway | He waits until the Vales' lamp goes out, gives Cass his turn ("Every two hours, left then right"), and walks down the road alone. Past the barricade. Dark houses, dogs that don't bark. | Decision: he chooses the person in front of him over the rule, the first time against Juno. |
| Wren's mother | He turns her, spoons water, washes her face. In the bedroom he finds a child's drawing on the fridge and stands in front of it too long. | A fridge again. Echo, unexplained. |
| Back | He's back before the four o'clock turn. Cass did his. Nobody asks. He doesn't tell Juno. | His first kept thing. The secrets ledger grows a line. |

**Must land:** "The road is the road" in Juno's on-duty voice, and Lonny not arguing, and going.

### Scene 3 — the second grave (day 12, dawn)

| Beat | What happens | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The turn | Six o'clock turn. Cass's mother is cold. The bag is half empty. Lonny is the one who finds her; Cass is asleep in the chair beside her. Lonny has to wake him. | The second bed death, someone's mother, with the someone awake and seventeen. |
| Cass | Cass doesn't shout at Lonny. He looks down the hill at Kit's yard and says, "Taxi man's breathing." Then he goes to find Juno. | The blame lands on Juno, offstage ([04](04-sleepers.md)). Lonny hears the shouting from the garden. |
| The strength | Carrying her down the garden to the lime tree, he doesn't need Tock on the other end. He notices. Tock notices. Neither says it. At four feet, where the rock stopped him for Mr. Hale, the spade goes on through. | Earth, deniable ([03](03-power-system.md)): a boy who doesn't need help, and rock that gives. No glow. |
| Sequel | Two graves under the lime tree, both dug by a Hale. In the Hales' kitchen he eats a whole loaf standing up and is still hungry, and for the first time wonders if that's a thing. | Earth's cost starting to show (hunger), read as his old wound. Let the reader suspect before he does. |
| Last beat | Lyra at the kitchen door with the little ones' list, taking the job before Juno can, because she watched him go down the road alone last night and nobody stopped him. | Lyra takes the little ones (old ch 11 beat). Someone saw him: his secret has a witness. |

**Must land:** waking Cass. The spade not stopping. The loaf.

### Setups this chapter pays or plants

- **Pays:** Judge's note and Fen's ship secret (ch 6, ch 10), Juno's ruling (ch 10), Lonny's yes to Wren (ch 4), Mr. Hale's grave and the rock at four feet (ch 5), Juno's "you can't take it back" (Cass answers it with a body).
- **Plants:** Cass as a rival inside the walls, Lonny on the road (he leaves for it in ch 17), Earth's hunger, Lyra and the little ones, Lyra knowing about the road, Tock knowing about the strength.
- **Not yet:** anyone rooting in secret (the Nettle girl can be seen at Kit's yard, nothing more); the twelve bags' source; Lonny saying any of it out loud.

### Who knows what after ch 11

Lonny knows about the ship (overheard). Lyra knows Lonny went down the road; Juno doesn't. Lonny and Tock know he carried a woman alone and the rock gave; neither has said so. The whole Trace knows Cass blames Juno.

### Checks before saving

- Three scenes; "said" only. Lonny's dialect: the heaviest of the six, warm, short sentences ([21](21-style-sheet.md)).
- His interior is simple and concrete: food, weight, what's in front of him. No metaphors he wouldn't use.
- His humour once, early, before the wheelbarrow ("I'm eating *near* it" territory). None after dawn.
- Juno is never the villain on the page. She's tired, counting, and right about the carers.
